# 镜头表 · Shot list — HOW THE MOON FORMED

Silent 8-bit pixel-art explainer, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 45 s. Music grid 128 BPM: 1 beat = 0.46875 s,
1 bar = 1.875 s, 24 bars in all. Every scene starts on a bar line, and the giant impact lands on the downbeat of bar 5
(9.375 s), which is the first drop. It shows the giant-impact hypothesis (a model, not an observed event), and the
timescales are compressed and uncertain.

The camera never cuts away from the material: molten proto-Earth → Theia falls in → impact → the ejected melt and
vapour → the debris disk → clumps merging → the molten young Moon → its crust → the modern Earth and Moon.
The impact, debris disk and accretion are driven by real particle simulations (2D SPH with self-gravity for the
collision, then an N-body run with merging for the Moon's accretion), drawn in pixel art.

画风：8-bit 像素画（270×480 低分辨率画、4 倍最近邻放大、全片一套限色调色板＋4×4 有序抖动），用 Kurzgesagt 的扁平分面（同色相深一档的阴影带＋受光边细高光，不描黑边）、发光体径向辉光、多层星空视差和对数尺度推拉来组织画面与运动
色板：#05060c #6e1a0c #f05a14 #ffc93a #8592b0 #2f6bff
角色：无

Camera words: 停 hold · 缓推 slow push · 拉远 pull out · 横移 pan/track · 快推 fast push (accent) · 砸入 slam · 切 cut.
