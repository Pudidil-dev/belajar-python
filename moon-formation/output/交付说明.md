## 制作说明（制作者填写；只写在这一段，事实段不要动）

- 用了哪个语法 / 风格：the y1 Kurzgesagt grammar drawn in 8-bit pixels, using the same 14_8bit pipeline as the previous film in this repo:
  - every frame drawn at 270×480, one palette, 4×4 Bayer dither, 4× nearest-neighbour upscale;
  - planets rendered by a per-pixel sphere shader with banded light, rim highlights, glow halos and dithered colour ramps;
  - parallax stars, log-scale zooms between scales (planet → plume → droplets and back), and a cutaway of the Moon.
  The impact, the debris and the accretion are drawn from two physics simulations: 2D SPH with self-gravity for the collision, and an N-body run with merging for the Moon. They play back through clocks that map film time to simulation time (slow motion at the impact, faster and faster through the accretion). There is one continuous camera over nine scenes cut on the bar lines of a 128 BPM grid. Shot list: `镜头表.md` (11 shots, lint green). No three-direction check was made, because the style direction is the one already used for the previous film in this repo, and only the subject is new.
