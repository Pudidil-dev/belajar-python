# 镜头表 · Shot list — HOW GAMMA KNIFE MACHINES WORK

*From radioactive decay to a planned dose deep in the brain, on one Leksell Gamma Knife Icon.* 90 seconds, 9:16, no
narration and no subtitles; small context labels on leader lines, taken 1:1 from the client's 5-page storyboard.
- **Format.** 1080×1920, 60 fps, exactly 90.000 s; 49 micro-scenes; every cut on the 16th-note grid of the score (120 BPM).
- **Timing.** The brief asked for 60 s; 49 scenes in 60 s is 1.2 s a scene, too compact and flashy to read the labels,
  so (at the client's request) each scene is lengthened to 1–2.5 s (1.8 s on average); the hook keeps the shortest beats.
- **Pixels.** Drawn at 360×640 and scaled exactly 3× with nearest-neighbour (finer than the X-ray film's 270×480 so the
  192 sources, the nuclei and the labels stay readable): flat shapes with a light edge and a shadow band, transparent
  cutaways, hard-edged particles, log-scale zooms between scales.
- **Machine.** One Leksell Gamma Knife Icon throughout: 192 sealed Co-60 sources in 8 motorized sectors of 24, a fixed
  tungsten collimator body with 4 / 8 / 16 mm channels and a shielded OFF position, a motorized couch, the integrated
  CBCT, the thermoplastic mask and infrared motion management with a nose marker.
- **Loop.** The last frame is the first frame: the same head, the same beams, the same pulse phase.

画风：8/16-bit 像素科普动画，继承本仓库 X 光那一集（平涂底色 + 左上亮边 + 右下暗带、透明剖面、硬边粒子、对数缩放转场），Kurzgesagt 式的清晰层次；360×640 画、3 倍最近邻放大
