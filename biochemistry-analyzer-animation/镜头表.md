# 镜头表 · How a Biochemistry Analyzer Measures Cholesterol

36 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM (one bar = 2 s, 18 bars = the whole loop; every hit sits on the 8th-note
grid). No title card, no narration, no subtitles. The only text is on the workstation display ("Total Cholesterol"
and one illustrative value in mg/dL).

The film follows one serum sample through one total cholesterol test. The 18 beats are connected by tracking moves,
zooms and match cuts:
- 0–4 s: a preview (drop → colour → reveal), then a match cut back to the empty cuvette;
- the chronological workflow catches up with the opening at 13.25 s and cuts straight to the pipette retracting;
- 34–36 s: the next cuvette lands on frame 0.

The hero cuvette has a subtle pale-gold rim highlight in every view. At 32.4 s the highlight passes to the fresh
cuvette at the sample position: it is the one the opening drop falls into when the loop starts again.

The sample sits as a thin gold layer on top of the reagent until the stirrer mixes it (14.35–15.55 s).

画风：16-bit pixel-art lab game (SNES era) with chunky 8-bit silhouettes: every frame drawn on a 270×480 canvas and scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; the analyzer is a 2D drawing in 3/4 top-down view (like the centrifuge film: it looks like a 3D pixel model but every part is drawn in 2D: cylinders, boxes and wedges with dithered ramps, 1-px outlines, bright top-front edges and contact shadows); separate 2D artwork per scale (macro, cutaway, molecular and optical views up close), each redrawn at its own zoom; flat dark navy background.
色板：navy（#0D1B2A #16293D #EEF3F6 #8FA3B3 #5CC8E0 #F2A93B）+ serum gold #E0C067 + rose dye #D45577
角色：无

色板 = the skill's `navy` set plus:
- the off-white of the analyzer body (ink #EEF3F6 family);
- pale-gold serum #E0C067;
- a rose reaction product #D45577 (hue ≈ 344°, outside the blue-purple zone).

The reagent arm wears the teal accent (#5CC8E0), the sample arm the amber accent (#F2A93B). Sample tubes (tall round
tubes), reagent bottles (wide wedge bottles with caps) and reaction cuvettes (small square cells) have different
shapes.

## 镜头表 lint 黄灯说明

- **⑥ Short beats** (0.65 s, 1.45 s, 1.9 s). This is the brief's own timing. The opening is a 4-second preview built
  from three beats that flow into each other (drop → colour → reveal) with no hard cuts between them.
- **Screen text.** "Total Cholesterol" and the value are on the workstation screen in the world, not a caption, as the
  brief asks.
- **⑬ Palette (8 colours).** The brief asks for these as separate colour roles: dark navy background, off-white
  machine body, pale-gold serum, rose reaction product, and two different arm accents (teal reagent arm, amber sample
  arm) so the two pipette paths are easy to tell apart. Everything else is shades of these.
