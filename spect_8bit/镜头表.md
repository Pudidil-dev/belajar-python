## Notes

- **Locked timing:** the brief's 27 time ranges, snapped to the 8th-note grid; key events sit on downbeats: the
  photoelectric absorption (bar 15, 26.44 s), the scintillation (bar 17, 30.22 s), the dynode hits (one per beat from
  36.36 s), the scope pulse (bar 22, 39.67 s), the energy sum (bar 24), the rejection (bar 25), the image slam (bar 34).
- **Orientation:** the scanner is seen from the foot of the table, so the detector heads turn around the chest as
  plain 2-D rotations. Inside the head everything keeps the way it hangs over the patient: the patient side is down,
  and the signal rises — collimator, crystal, light guide, photomultipliers, electronics (the brief's order). Panels
  10, 11, 15–17 are redrawn in that orientation.
- **Scanner style (user feedback):** the first 3-D version of the scanner looked wrong, so the scanner and the detector
  head are flat 2-D pixel art (no perspective mesh): a true-circle gantry, two rounded heads with blue faces, the patient
  in layered silhouettes, a flat cut-away of the head.
- **Camera:** holds while a mechanism plays; pushes are answered by pull-outs; three accents only (the rush into the
  chest, the dive into the nucleus, the image slam). The photon is followed through every scale.
- **Text:** one short caption at a time, top-left; short leader-line tags next to the thing they name; one disclaimer
  chip at a time (not to scale, time-lapse, illustrative colours, real scan longer).
- **Scale:** cells, molecules, nuclei and particles are magnified and coloured for teaching.
- **Data:** projections, counts, the reconstruction and the perfusion slices are computed from one simulated tracer
  distribution (parallel-hole projections with attenuation, counting noise, slice-by-slice MLEM), not painted.
- **Lint (storyboard_lint):** run before the code: no red; yellow only for the short 1.89 s shots, which the brief fixes.
