## Notes

- **Sequences, not cuts:** 7 sequences; the only hard cuts are the match cut from the needle to the display (0.8 s),
  the cut to the hemoglobin aliquot (27 s) and the cut into the WBC channel (32 s). Everything else is camera
  tracking, pushes and pull-backs on the same object, joined by smooth zoom crossfades (no portals, no flashes).
- **Pacing:** the impedance mechanism is held for 8 s on one locked view (approach 3 s, entry 3 s, exit 2 s); the
  laser crossing holds 4 s, then a 3 s slow-motion view. Labels wait until the physical change has happened.
- **Aliquots:** the red cell counted by impedance, the cells lysed for hemoglobin and the white cell analysed by laser
  come from separate measured aliquots of the same sample, never one cell passing through every pathway.
- **Scale:** red cell ~7.5 µm, platelet ~2.5 µm, white cells 10–15 µm with nuclei; the aperture is wider than a cell.
- **Text:** one short caption at a time near the mechanism; leader-line tags; one honesty chip at a time (EXAMPLE
  VALUES, SLOWED DOWN, SIMPLIFIED, NOT TO SCALE, COLOURS ARE SOFTWARE GROUPS); captions and chips leave after ~3 s.
- **Colour code:** red cells crimson, white cells off-white with purple nuclei, platelets violet, plasma amber,
  electrolyte soft cyan, electrodes silver, current thin cyan, pulses neon green, laser red, scatter orange / cyan,
  fluorescence green, photodetectors metallic blue, data cyan / white.
