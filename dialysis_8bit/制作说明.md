# Production notes (制作说明) — How Dialysis Machines Clean Your Blood

These notes go into `output/交付说明.md` under the "制作说明" heading. deliver.py writes the facts section itself.

## What was used

- **Skill tier**: open tier (开阔档), so I wrote my own renderer in Python/numpy/Pillow instead of the
  Canvas engine. The skill's gates were still followed: shot list + lint, three directions, film_gate,
  independent review, deliver.py.
- **Grammar**: y1 Kurzgesagt scale travel.
  - arm → machine: fast pull-out reveal with a long tail
  - machine → dialyzer: push-through with a colour match, then pull-out
  - dialyzer → fiber: lens reveal (the fiber lights up first, ≥0.3 s)
  - fiber → membrane: slow push
  - membrane → fiber segment: pull-out
  - fiber → arm: fast pull-out
- **Look**: drawn in the 14_8bit pipeline. Low-res canvas 270×480, ×4 nearest-neighbour, parts outlined in their own darker hue
  (the skill's per-part outline idea, but same-hue rather than black, which suits the Kurzgesagt grammar).
- **Palette**: `navy` plus the brief's subject colour code. Blue-purple is used only for small albumin particles,
  as the brief asks for purple proteins.
- **Character**: only the patient's forearm and hand (no face, no code-drawn person).
- **Direction gate**: three compositions of the opening frame (`方向/`). The user picked **C, macro access**.
- **Audio**: 120 BPM grid (beat 0.5 s, 27 bars = 54 s), so the loop closes on a bar line. Every SFX comes from
  the same `timeline.events()` list as the picture: urea ticks at scheduled crossing times, bounce clicks,
  whooshes on transitions, cut hits in the montage, the title impact. Loudness about −15 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP.

## Lint yellows and why

- ⑥ shot 17 is 0.5 s: this is deliberate. It is the loop hand-off, the opening frame on the loop clock, so the
  file loops seamlessly.
- ⑬ albumin #7D4BC9 is blue-purple: the brief asks for purple proteins. They are small particles only, and the film
  gate measured a blue-purple share of 0.000.
- ⑬ ten colours: the navy base set plus the brief's five subject colours (blood, dialysate, urea, water, albumin),
  which the science needs.

## Six failure modes, self-check

1. **PPT page-flip**: no page frame or page numbers, and camera moves change from shot to shot (track, pull-out,
   push-through, lens, stop, glide, slow push, back-out, cuts). No three push-ins in a row. The ending lands on the
   arm with blood flowing, with the title over a live scene, not a centred card.
2. **Copying demos**: compositions and objects were designed for this subject. No demo assets are used.
3. **No concrete object**: every shot has a physical object (arm, needle, tube, pump, dialyzer, fiber, molecules).
4. **Dead background**: parallax dust, grid and bokeh in macro shots; flowing dialysate streaks, far fibers and
   neighbour fibers in micro shots. The gate measured a dead-background share of 0.11.
5. **Drawing in code when real footage exists**: no real footage was supplied, and the brief asks for pixel art.
6. **AI look**: one style for the whole film, navy (not blue-purple) ground, no neon purple, no code-drawn people,
   and molecules have no faces.
