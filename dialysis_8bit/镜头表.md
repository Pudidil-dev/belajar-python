## Notes

- The brief's final title sits on shot 16, a live arm shot with blood flowing under it, not on a static card. The last
  0.5 s (shot 17) clears the title and lands on the frame-0 framing so the clip loops. Shot 17 is shorter than 2 s
  on purpose, as the loop hand-off.
- Shot 1 keeps the brief's "BLOOD OUT" label, which arrives after the picture has started (~0.6 s). The optional
  caption is dropped so the hook is picture-led.
- Micro-captions were trimmed or shortened to stay within the skill's ≤8 words per shot and ≤30% text-led shots.
  Three are shorter than the brief's suggested wording: "Cleaned outside the body.", "Thousands of tiny fibers." and
  "Blood and dialysate stay apart."
- Scale travel: arm → machine is a pull-out. Machine → dialyzer is a push-through with colour fill and a pull-out.
  Dialyzer → fiber is a lens reveal. Fiber → membrane is a slow push. Membrane → fiber is a pull-out.
  Fiber → arm is a fast pull-out reveal. Push-ins are never chained three in a row.
- Music: 120 BPM, a 0.5 s beat, 27 bars = 54 s. Transitions start on beat points, and every SFX comes from the same event list as the picture.
