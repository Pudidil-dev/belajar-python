# 镜头表 · What Happens When You Distill Grape Juice?

32 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM, 16 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2 s; the main hits sit on the beat grid). One grape, one round-bottom distilling flask, one heater, one condenser, one receiver, start to finish. The flask is always on the left, the receiver on the right, the flow always left to right. Captions are small, clean labels (5×7 pixel font, white with a dark outline, no box) that sit next to the object they explain and move with it. The film ends by pushing in on the next hanging drop, which fades into the grape through an 8-bit dither, so the last frame flows into the first.

画风：2D 8-bit RPG lab pixel art: a 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; bold silhouettes, stepped 1-px outlines, a limited flat palette (at most a checker dither), small animated glints on the glass. Game-feel motion: squash and stretch, parts dropping into place on the beat, whole-pixel scrolling, a short screen shake on impacts, ambient sprites stepping on twos. The apparatus is drawn in world millimetres at each camera scale, so the wide view, the close-ups, the heater x-ray and the condenser cutaway each show their own detail instead of enlarging one image. No 3D, no soft gradients.
色板：#0E1B30 #5A1450 #9B5FD0 #1F9EC4 #FFFFFF #F2B347 #D8641C #FFF35C
允许蓝紫：the brief asks for violet highlights on the grapes; the juice itself is a red-leaning grape purple (hue ~308°)
角色：无

色板 = dark navy background, deep grape purple for the juice, violet highlights for the grapes, cyan only for the cooling water, white edge highlights for the clear droplets and the captions, amber only for the heat cue, orange for the hot coil, yellow only for the electric current inside the heater x-ray. The glass is pale grey-white edges over the navy; the heating mantle is grey with a red indicator.
