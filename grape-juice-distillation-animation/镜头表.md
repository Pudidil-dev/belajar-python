# 镜头表 · What Happens When You Distill Grape Juice?

26 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM, 13 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2 s; the main hits sit on the beat grid). No title card, narration, subtitles, floating HUD or decorative particles. One grape, one round-bottom distilling flask, one condenser, one receiver, start to finish. The flask is always on the left, the receiver on the right, the flow always left to right. The last frame is the next hanging drop at the grape's exact size and place; the video cuts back to the grape on the downbeat and loops.

画风：2D 8-bit RPG lab pixel art: a 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; bold silhouettes, stepped 1-px outlines, a limited flat palette (at most a checker dither for glass), small animated glints on the glass. The apparatus is drawn in world millimetres at each camera scale, so the wide view, the close-ups and the condenser cutaway each show their own detail instead of enlarging one image. No 3D, no soft gradients.
色板：#0E1B30 #5A1450 #9B5FD0 #1F9EC4 #FFFFFF #F2B347
允许蓝紫：the brief asks for violet highlights on the grapes; the juice itself is a red-leaning grape purple (hue ~308°)
角色：无

色板 = dark navy background, deep grape purple for the juice, violet highlights for the grapes, cyan only for the cooling water, white edge highlights for the clear droplets, amber only for the heat cue. The glass is pale grey-white edges over the navy; the heating mantle is grey with a red indicator.
