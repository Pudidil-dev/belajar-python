# 镜头表 · How Bacterial Colonies Grow on Agar

20 s seamless loop, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM (one bar = 2 s, 10 bars). One continuous camera journey with
no cuts and no transitions. It rides beside a cotton swab streaking a Petri dish, dives into the trail down to a
single bacterium, watches it split, and keeps pulling back while the cells keep doubling until they form one cream
colony. It tilts up to see the whole plate, where colonies pop up along the swab's path, and follows the swab as it
picks up our colony and streaks a fresh plate. The last frame runs straight into the first (same speed, direction
and place on screen), so there is no hidden cut. No title card, subtitles or voiceover. The only text is a tiny
TIME-LAPSE tag and the cell counter (`--clean` leaves both out).

One example runs through the whole film: one bacterium, left on the agar by the swab, becoming one colony.

画风：clean 2D 8-bit pixel art on a 270×480 canvas, upscaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour: 1-px outlines, flat pixel-cluster shading with Bayer dither, a 2.5D camera that can look from agar level up to straight down; the dish, the agar, the swab, the bacteria and the colonies are drawn at whatever scale the camera reaches, so the dive and the pull-out are one continuous move
色板：dark teal lab #0E2A2C + pale amber agar #E9BD6E + cream colony #EADCAE + swab white #FFFDF6 + mint target #6BD59B
角色：无（只有棉签、培养皿、细菌和菌落，不出人和手）
