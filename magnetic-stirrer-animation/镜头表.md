# 镜头表 · How a Magnetic Stirrer Spins Without Touching

24 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM (one bar = 2 s, 12 bars). No title card, no narration, no subtitles, no
floating diagrams. The only letters are printed on the props in the world: the flask label (CaCO₃), the bottle label
(VINEGAR) and the N / S on the magnet.

The film follows one object: the **white stir bar**. It lands in the empty beaker, the cloudy suspension hides it, a
magnet nobody touches makes it spin, vinegar reacts with the suspended chalk, and the same bar comes back into view
through clear liquid. That before-and-after is the payoff.

One beaker, one stir bar, one viewing direction (a slight top-down front view) from the first frame to the last. The
camera only moves twice:
- a gentle push in when the bubbles appear;
- a slow pull back while the liquid clears.

The cutaway opens with a horizontal pixel wipe while the beaker stays exactly where it is.

画风：16-bit pixel-art lab game (SNES era) with chunky 8-bit silhouettes: every frame drawn on a 270×480 canvas and scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; the beaker, stir bar and stirrer are 2D drawings in a slight top-down front view (round parts as ellipses, dithered 16-bit ramps, 1-px outlines, bright rim highlights), redrawn at each zoom; flat dark navy background.
色板：navy #0D1B2A + steel gray #8FA3B3 + off-white #EEF3F6 + cloudy gray #C9CFD4 + magnet red #E0533D + amber #F2A93B
角色：无

色板 roles:
- navy: background and the stirrer's dark front panel;
- steel gray: glass, the top plate, the motor;
- off-white: the stir bar and the stirrer body;
- cloudy gray: the calcium carbonate suspension;
- magnet red: the N pole;
- amber: the knob pointer, the power light and the glints.

Everything else is a shade of these.
