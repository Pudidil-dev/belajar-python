# 镜头表 · Surgical Light, Fully Functional (3D)

32.5 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 96 BPM, 13 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2.5 s). One surgical light in one operating room, in 3D, one camera that never cuts away. The client's brief: the hook shows the operation for one second from the side, then the camera goes to the lamp and straight into how it works. The client then asked for 3D, for the lamp seen from the front, and for the light's effects on the operation, the hands included. So each bar shows one function and what it does to the operation: the face of the lamp with its LEDs and their lenses, the rays meeting at one spot, the hands' shadows going soft as the LEDs come on, the sensors working round a head in the way, brightness, the warm and cool LEDs mixing the colour, the size of the lit spot, endo mode, where the heat goes (a heat view), the sterile handle and its camera on the monitor, and the spring arm. The camera then comes back to the opening shot, and the last frame runs into the first. No narration: clean captions name the things on screen (white text and a thin pointer line), in English.

画风：hybrid 3D and 2D 8-bit pixel art: the operating room is a 3D scene (ray-cast in code, lit by every LED of the lamp with real shadows) drawn on a 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; every pixel snapped to one fixed 8-bit palette (nearest colour, a light ordered dither only at the transitions), 1-px outlines where objects meet; the rays, glows, scan lines and heat specks are 2D pixel effects on top; no game elements (no HUD, titles, scores or player tags).
色板：#CFE2DE #2C7A86 #3E8C84 #EEF2F1 #FFE8CC #E2ECFA
角色：two surgeons in teal gowns, caps and masks (only their eyes show), working at the table; the surgical light is the main object.

色板 = pale teal-grey walls, teal-blue sterile drapes, teal-green gowns, the lamp's white shell, warm-white and cool-white light (the two kinds of LED). Supporting tones: steel for the table and the arm, cream gloves, the prepped skin in the drape window, cyan lenses, a mint sensor light and scan, a dark monitor; in the heat view, teal (cool) to orange and white (hot).
