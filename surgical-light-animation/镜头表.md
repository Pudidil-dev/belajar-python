# 镜头表 · Surgical Light, Fully Functional

32.5 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 96 BPM, 13 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2.5 s). One surgical light, one camera that never cuts away. The client's brief: the hook shows the operation for 1 second from the side, then the camera zooms into the lamp and goes straight into how the lamp works. From there each bar shows one thing the light does, in an x-ray (cutaway) view of the lamp head: its LEDs and their lenses, all beams aimed at one spot, why a head in the way leaves no hard shadow, the sensors that shift the power, brightness, colour temperature, the size of the light field, endo mode, the heat sink, the sterile handle with its camera, and the spring arm. The camera then pulls back out to the operating room, and the last frame runs into the first. No narration: clean captions name the things on screen (white text and a thin pointer line), in English.

画风：2D 8-bit pixel art, a side view of an operating room and an x-ray cutaway of the surgical light; 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; stepped 1-px outlines, a limited flat palette, at most a checker or ordered dither; no game elements (no HUD, titles, scores or player tags).
色板：#CFE2DE #2C7A86 #3E8C84 #EEF2F1 #FFE8C2 #57B07A
角色：two surgeons in teal gowns, caps and masks (only their eyes show), working at the table; the surgical light is the main object.

色板 = pale teal-grey operating-room walls, teal-blue sterile drapes, teal-green gowns, the lamp's white shell, warm-white light, and the green circuit boards seen in the x-ray. Supporting tones: steel for the table and the arm, cyan lenses, an orange heat glow, a red sensor light, a dark monitor.
