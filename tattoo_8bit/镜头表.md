# How Lasers Remove Tattoos — shot list (镜头表)

Project: 9:16, logical 360×640 ×3 → 1080×1920, 60 fps, exactly 48.000 s (2,880 frames), 20 micro-scenes grouped in
5 continuous sequences (A hook 0–4, B how the laser breaks the ink 4–19, C immune cells remove pigment 19–33,
D fading over time 33–40, E final time-lapse 40–48). Micro-scenes inside a sequence join by camera moves and soft zoom
crossfades, not cuts; the time jumps are labelled (WEEKS LATER, NEXT SESSION, SESSION 1 … FINAL RESULT).
Music grid: 120 BPM, 24 bars of 4/4 = 48.000 s. Every physical event sits on the eighth-note grid (15 frames), so the
picture and its sound effect land on the same frame: the four laser pulses of the hook at 0.25–1.0 s (4 Hz, a real
Q-switched repetition rate), the fragmentation split on the downbeat of bar 8 (14.0 s), the phagocytosis closure on
the downbeat of bar 13 (24.0 s), the final result on the downbeat of bar 23 (44.0 s).
Skill: huashu-art-motion, open tier (own renderer). Grammar: y1 Kurzgesagt scale travel. One example from start to
finish: one forearm, one black dragon tattoo in line work (changed from the brief's star at the user's request), one
Q-switched Nd:YAG 1064 nm handpiece, one ink aggregate followed through fragmentation, its fragments followed into
one macrophage, one lymphatic vessel.

画风：16-bit high-detail pixel art in Kurzgesagt flat-science grammar: dark navy ground, flat 2–3 tone shading with same-hue dark edges, soft smooth glows only on light sources (no flashes, no explosions, no plasma balls), one style in every shot

## Notes

- **One subject per transformation:** the camera follows one ink aggregate from the dermis view to its fragments,
  then some of those fragments into one macrophage and some into one lymphatic vessel.
- **Frosting is not removal:** the white of the hook is gas bubbles in the treated skin and is gone by the time jump;
  the micro view shows the same bubbles forming around the aggregate.
- **Time is labelled:** the pulse itself lasts nanoseconds (SLOW MOTION chip during absorption, expansion and
  fragmentation); clearance takes weeks (WEEKS LATER, the timeline in the payoff); the final clear skin is an
  illustrative outcome (RESULTS VARY).
- **Light:** 1064 nm is invisible; the beam is drawn as a faint violet-red educational overlay, never a bright beam
  (chip: INVISIBLE IN REALITY).
- **Text:** one short caption at a time in a solid box, small leader-line tags on the things they name, one honesty
  chip at a time; captions and chips leave after about 3 s.
- **Colour code:** skin warm tones, epidermis peach, dermis pink, ink deep black, laser faint violet-red,
  macrophages pale white-blue with deep purple nuclei, lymphatic vessels soft green, the laser console's electronics
  cyan, ground dark navy.
- **Loop:** the payoff timeline rewinds (labelled) to the first frame: the same forearm, the same dragon, the handpiece
  back in place, the next pulse on the next eighth note.
