# 镜头表 · How CRISPR-Cas9 Edits DNA

22 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM, 11 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2 s; one beat = exactly 15 frames). Inside a cell nucleus, one DNA double helix, one Cas9 protein carrying its guide RNA, start to finish. The hook shows the whole cut in three seconds (WHOOSH, CLICK, SNIP) and rushes into the break; a tape rewind takes us back, the helix straightens into a ladder, and Cas9 (seen x-ray) slides along it: it stops at a PAM where the sequence is wrong, moves on, stops at the right PAM, the DNA unwinds and the 20 guide bases pair with the target strand one by one, glowing, until Cas9 locks (TIK, TIK, LOCK). Closer, its two cutting domains (HNH on the paired strand, RuvC on the other) cut both strands 3 base pairs before the PAM (SNIP) and in slow motion the ends part. Cas9 lets go; the cell's repair proteins grab the two ends, trim them (2 base pairs are lost) and a ligase seals the strands (TIK, TIK, SNAP). The before and after DNA side by side show the missing base pairs (DNA EDITED), and another Cas9 heads for a new helix: the last frame is the first. No narration and no subtitles; four tiny labels (CAS9 · GUIDE RNA, PAM, REPAIR, DNA EDITED).

画风：2D 8-bit pixel art in a retro RPG game style: a 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; every sprite a short ramp (outline, dark, mid, light, highlight) with 1-px dark outlines, flat fills and only small dither patches; the DNA, Cas9 and the repair proteins are drawn again at the camera's scale on every frame, so zooms stay crisp; bright molecules on a dark navy nucleus; hard-edged pixel effects (glows on paired bases, impact flashes, sparks, a tape rewind); tiny 5×7 pixel labels only.
色板：#0B1B32 #3FC8E0 #FF5FA8 #E0661C #5CC428 #F5C431
角色：无（no people and no faces: the proteins are plain molecular shapes）

色板 = the navy nucleus (hue ~214, outside the blue-violet band), cyan DNA backbones, the hot-pink guide RNA, the orange Cas9 protein, the lime repair rings, and gold for the ligase and the effects. The four bases are colour-coded (A green, T red, G yellow, C blue) and RNA has U (orange) instead of T; the two cutting domains are gold (HNH) and teal (RuvC).
