# 镜头表 · How a Blood Typing Card Reads Your Blood

24 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM (one bar = 2 s, 12 bars; the end returns to the first falling drop on the
next downbeat). No title card and no narration. Text in the world: the four labels printed on the card (Anti-A,
Anti-B, Anti-D, Control) and the result line "His blood type is A".

Explanation layer (user requests: "clean captions for each explanation", then "tracking explanations, not a caption
at the top"): short labels on small dark plates, pinned by dotted leader lines to the things they explain and
following them through every camera move. `--clean` renders the brief's text-free cut (card labels + result line
only); `--lang id` writes the labels in Bahasa Indonesia.

Red blood cells (user request: "a more correct cell shape"): biconcave discs with the Evans–Fung thickness profile,
flat 8-bit shading with the reversed shading in the dent; free cells tumble (tilted ovals, edge-on bars).

One example runs through the whole film: four drops of the same blood on one test card. Only the Anti-A spot clumps.
Inside that drop one antibody bridges two red blood cells, and many bridges make the clump you can see. The same
cells in Anti-B stay apart. The letter A appears only after that.

画风：2D 8-bit RPG lab: chunky pixel sprites with stepped 1-px outlines on a 270×480 canvas, upscaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; flat pixel-cluster shading (no gradients, no glow); separate artwork for the overhead card, the close-up drop and the cellular view, redrawn at each zoom; camera positions on the pixel grid when the camera rests.
色板：navy #0D1B2A + ivory #F5EAD3 + blood red #B3262E + dark red #8E1B23 + gold #F2B33D + cyan #3FC6DC
角色：无
