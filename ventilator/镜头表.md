# 镜头表 · Shot list — HOW MEDICAL VENTILATORS WORK

*One mechanically delivered breath, from electronic control to the alveoli and back.* 88 seconds, 9:16, no narration.
Small explanatory labels name the parts, molecules and tissues (a white name, one grey line on what it does), at the
client's request in the revision; the ventilator's own screen shows its waveforms and numbers.
- **Format.** 1080×1920, 60 fps, exactly 88.000 s; 40 micro-scenes; every cut on the 16th-note grid of the score (120 BPM).
- **Revision.** Calmer: each scene runs about 1.5× longer (the film went from 60 to 88 s), no rapid zoom bursts, small
  rings and soft signal glows; round gas particles; organic airways and alveolar ducts, wavy cell layers, an edge-on
  biconcave red cell, a natural face (eyes closed); the hook is now one continuous shot of the lungs inflating and
  deflating, without cuts or flashes.
- **Pixels.** Drawn at 270×480 and scaled exactly 4× with nearest-neighbour — the visual language of the X-ray film: flat
  shapes with a light edge and a shadow band, cutaways, hard-edged particles, log-scale zooms between scales.
- **Ventilation model.** Volume assist-control; a time-triggered mandatory breath (no patient effort); a square inspiratory
  flow to a target tidal volume (450 mL); passive expiration; PEEP 5 cmH2O; FiO2 40 % from an air/oxygen blend; a heated
  humidifier; a dual-limb circuit with a Y-connector and an endotracheal tube. One representative configuration.
- **Loop.** The last frame is the first frame: the same camera, lungs at rest, the next breath's gas arriving, the same
  particle phases; frame 0 is the moment the next breath starts.

画风：Kurzgesagt 式科普动画 × 简单 2D 像素，继承 X 光那一集的画法（270×480 画、4 倍最近邻放大、限色色板；每个零件一块平涂底色，左上 1 像素亮边，右下一条暗带；剖面、硬边粒子、对数缩放转场）

circuit board green with copper traces.
