# 镜头表 · Shot list — HOW MEDICAL VENTILATORS WORK

*One mechanically delivered breath, from electronic control to the alveoli and back.* 60 seconds, 9:16, no narration, no
subtitles: the visuals explain it, and the only text is the ventilator's own screen (its waveforms and numbers).
- **Format.** 1080×1920, 60 fps, exactly 60.000 s; 40 micro-scenes; every cut on the 16th-note grid of the score (120 BPM).
- **Pixels.** Drawn at 270×480 and scaled exactly 4× with nearest-neighbour — the visual language of the X-ray film: flat
  shapes with a light edge and a shadow band, cutaways, hard-edged particles, log-scale zooms between scales.
- **Ventilation model.** Volume assist-control; a time-triggered mandatory breath (no patient effort); a square inspiratory
  flow to a target tidal volume (450 mL); passive expiration; PEEP 5 cmH2O; FiO2 40 % from an air/oxygen blend; a heated
  humidifier; a dual-limb circuit with a Y-connector and an endotracheal tube. One representative configuration.
- **Loop.** The last frame is the first frame: the same camera, lung volume, pulse position and particle phases.

画风：Kurzgesagt 式科普动画 × 简单 2D 像素，继承 X 光那一集的画法（270×480 画、4 倍最近邻放大、限色色板；每个零件一块平涂底色，左上 1 像素亮边，右下一条暗带；剖面、硬边粒子、对数缩放转场）
色板：#05080f #3fd8e8 #d9a24a #f0d43a #d8333a #e07a6a
角色：只出手和背影（病人只画成透明解剖剖面和不带五官的头部，不画脸）

Colour code (the brief's): background dark navy · ventilator light grey / white · mechanics steel grey · inspiratory gas
cyan trails · expiratory gas muted amber · O₂ cyan-blue pairs · N₂ grey pairs · CO₂ small amber triads · water vapour pale ·
electrical signals bright yellow · blood dark to brighter red (never blue) · airway walls coral · alveoli soft pink ·
circuit board green with copper traces.

Camera words: 停 hold · 缓推 slow push · 拉远 pull out · 横移 track / pan · 快推 fast push (accent) · 切 cut.
