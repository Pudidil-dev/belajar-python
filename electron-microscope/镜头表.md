# 镜头表 · Shot list — HOW ELECTRON MICROSCOPES SEE ATOMS

Silent 8-bit pixel-art explainer, 1080×1920 vertical, 60 fps, exactly 115 s. Music grid 120 BPM: 1 beat = 0.5 s,
1 bar = 2 s, 230 beats. Every scene boundary of the brief (0, 4, 9, 16, 23, 30, 38, 45, 52, 59, 66, 74, 83, 91, 99,
108, 115 s) falls on a beat; the climax lands on the finished atomic image (99–108 s) and the final reveal.

One connected journey, out and back in: ONE GOLD ATOM in a HAADF-STEM micrograph → the micrograph on the workstation
monitor → the lab and the microscope → the cutaway column (gun, accelerator, condenser lenses, aberration corrector,
scan coils, specimen, detectors, pumps) → the specimen grid going in → the column pumped down → the tungsten tip and
field emission → 200 kV acceleration → the electron's wavelength → a magnetic lens → aberration correction and the
atom-sized probe → the raster scan → one electron scattering off one gold atom → gold vs carbon → the HAADF detector
(scintillator → light → photomultiplier → signal) → the image built pixel by pixel → ONE GOLD ATOM again (loop).

The micrograph is synthesized from the same physics the film explains: isolated gold atoms (and one small cluster) on a
3 nm amorphous carbon film, each atom imaged as the probe intensity (~0.07 nm) convolved with its scattering strength
(∝ Z^1.7, gold Z = 79, carbon Z = 6), then shot noise and scan-line jitter. 0.025 nm per image pixel, 6.75 × 12 nm
field of view, a 1 nm scale bar. The user's reference figures set its look: bright Gaussian spots on dark speckle, a
dashed cursor ring on one atom, a line profile with a Gaussian fit, atom-finding circles, HAADF bright vs BF dark.

Physics fixed for the whole film: aberration-corrected STEM at 200 kV (λ = 2.51 pm, v = 0.70 c); cold field-emission
gun (tungsten tip, radius ~100 nm, field ~5 V/nm, barrier ~1 nm thick, work function ~4.5 eV); vacuum ~10⁻⁵ Pa in the
column, ~10⁻⁹ Pa at the gun; magnetic lenses (coils, iron pole pieces; the electrons spiral as they focus); spherical
aberration of round lenses corrected by multipoles; probe ~0.2 nm before correction, ~0.07 nm after (illustrative);
probe current ~30 pA = about one electron in the column at a time, hundreds per pixel; HAADF annulus ~70–200 mrad,
low-angle electrons pass through its hole; scintillator → light guide → photomultiplier → amplifier → ADC; each scan
position gives one pixel. Beam damage is shown once (an atom hops under the beam).

画风：8-bit / 16-bit 像素画（270×480 低分辨率画、4 倍最近邻放大、全片一套限色调色板＋4×4 有序抖动），Kurzgesagt 式扁平分面、硬边发光、剖面揭开、对数尺度推拉；显微像是按物理合成的 HAADF 灰度像（探针卷积＋泊松噪声＋扫描行抖动）
