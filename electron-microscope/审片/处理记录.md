# What was done with each review finding

The review is `审片.md`, written by an independent agent from the first full render (`film_v1.mp4`). This file records, for each of its findings, what changed in the delivered film, or why nothing did. Times are film seconds.

## Must fix

**M1. Captions are erased before they can be read.** Fixed.
- **Typing and exit.** Captions now type at 60 characters/s and leave with a 0.25 s dithered fade instead of un-typing.
- **Retiming.** Scenes 5–15 were retimed so that every line finishes typing and holds. A new tool, `tools/caption_audit.py`, renders each scene at 30 fps and measures, for every text, the time between fully typed and leaving. It compares that against subtitle reading speed: 17 characters/s from when the text starts, plus 0.3 s to find it.
- **The four lines the review named:**
  - "ELECTRONS HAVE WAVELENGTHS" holds about 1.5 s.
  - "~200,000x SHORTER THAN LIGHT" is now a label inside the electron panel, and each panel carries a permanent "NOT TO SCALE".
  - "BUT LENS FLAWS ALSO LIMIT DETAIL" holds through the move back to the column.
  - "NO GLASS - ONLY A MAGNETIC FIELD" holds about 1.7 s. The coil-current meter moved up beside the coil so these lines no longer have to give way to it.
- **Other lines.** "THE PROBE MOVES - THE SAMPLE STAYS" now runs from the side view into the top view (about 2 s). "ALL RAYS MEET", "PROBE SIZE: ~0.07 nm" with its "ILLUSTRATIVE BEST-CASE VALUE" line, and "ATOMIC-RESOLUTION IMAGE" all complete and hold.
- **Still short.** A handful of lines remain 0.1–0.3 s short of the strict rule. They are listed in the delivery note.

**M2. Full-screen gold flash at 20.43 s and 23.60–23.85 s.** Fixed.
- **Cause.** In the dive, gold atoms switched on at about 300 nm field of view. They were drawn as 1-px dots at the micrograph's density, about 10⁵ of them, which filled the frame.
- **Fix.** Atoms now fade in only below a ~40 nm field, where single atoms can be told apart. The carbon network's atom dots fade in instead of popping.
- **Measurement.** The largest frame-to-frame jump in mean luma across 18.5–24.5 s is now 20/255, against 77/255 before. What remains are the designed crossfades between zoom levels.

**M3. High-angle scattering overstated about tenfold.** Fixed; I agree with the estimate.
- **Sampling.** Impact points are now sampled from the probe's profile in 3D (σ ≈ 0.032 nm, against the atom's 0.15 nm) and seen in projection. Only passes within about 0.01 nm of the nucleus count as high-angle, about 4 %.
- **Scattering scene.** The counter ends at "HIGH-ANGLE: 3 / 72 ELECTRONS".
- **Z-contrast scene.** The same 150 electrons go to each atom: gold scores 6, carbon 0. Z^1.7 predicts about 0.08 for carbon.
- **Detector scene.** About 6 % of electrons now reach the ring.

## Should fix

**S1. Large deflections drawn as hits on the nucleus.** Fixed.
- The starburst at the closest approach is gone. Every trajectory is a continuous curve that stays visible throughout.
- A close pass brightens the atom's equipotential rings for a moment.
- The audio accent and a whole-pixel camera shake land on the deflection.

**S2. Colour gives away the outcome, and the detector geometry does not match the counter.** Fixed.
- Electrons are cyan until their close pass, and only high-angle ones turn gold after it.
- In the Z-contrast scene the incoming probe is cyan in both panels.
- A hit counts only if the path lands on a detector bar. The bars now span the drawn high-angle range, and the forced close passes are aimed at 30–42°, so all of them land on a bar.

**S3. Gold atoms drawn as light sources.** Fixed.
- Gold atoms are now solid balls lit from the upper left, with a dark rim and no glow. This applies to the specimen slab, the dive, the wavelength panels and the raster-scan view.
- Glows are kept for the probe, detector hits and image pixels.

**S4. The second half barely moves.** Partly addressed, by design.
- **Added:**
  - impact shakes on the scattering events;
  - a shock ring when the gold pixel lands;
  - the HAADF/BF inset;
  - a shorter, cleaner return out of the tip and the film.
- **Left as it was:** I did not add slow 2–5 % pushes to the diagrams. The skill's director notes, from its author, warn that a uniform slow push on every shot reads as a slideshow, and that the camera should hold while viewers read a diagram.
- **Gate:** `film_gate` still reports 75–105 s as slow-moving (yellow). The delivery note explains why.

**S5. Leader lines through text; a label on the column pointing off screen.** Fixed.
- **Leader lines.** A leader now stops at the edge of its label box nearest the anchor, so it never crosses the words.
- **Control electronics.** The lab pull-back ends slightly further right, so the first electronics rack is in frame. "CONTROL / ELECTRONICS" points at it from the end of scene 2 into scene 3.
- **Margins.** The right-hand summary labels and the beam label moved in from the edge (16 and 14 art px).
- **Platform safe zones:** not applied. The brief names no platform.

**S6. Gun at atmospheric pressure; ion pumps working from 1 atm.** Fixed.
- The gun stays sealed behind its closed valve at about 10⁻⁹ Pa ("GUN: ~10⁻⁹ Pa", "GUN VALVE CLOSED"). Gas fills only the column below the valve.
- The turbo pump works first. The column's ion pumps start glowing below about 10⁻² Pa ("ION PUMPS ON").
- The valve opens at 10⁻⁵ Pa ("VALVE OPENS"), then the camera rises to the gun.
- The gauge starts at "1×10⁵ Pa = 1 atm", and its ticks no longer touch the exponents.

**S7. Light-vs-electron comparison far off scale.** Partly addressed.
- The light wave is drawn about 2.5× longer: both atoms now sit under one crest, and a line gives the atom gap ("ATOMS 0.29 nm APART").
- The numbers on screen carry the real ratio (λ ≈ 500 nm against 0.0025 nm), and "NOT TO SCALE" stays on each panel throughout.
- The true ratio of about 1,700 cannot be drawn at this size.

**S8. Caveat tags too faint.** Fixed. "wave amplitude exaggerated", "angles exaggerated", "ILLUSTRATIVE BEST-CASE VALUE" and "other detector types exist" now use the lighter gray (#a1a5aa on navy).

**S9. Two reference images unused.** Partly addressed.
- **Reference 3.** During the detector scene, two tiles show the same 20-atom raft as each detector records it: bright on dark (HAADF) and dark on bright (BF), labelled "SAME ATOMS".
- **Reference 1.** The crystal-column note was not added. The specimen in this film is single atoms on amorphous carbon, not a crystal.

**S10. Powers-of-ten zoom used three times; the rewind is unreadable.**
- The sample dive is kept.
- The rewind out of the film is now a short pull-back to about 1 µm, then a dissolve to the column.
- The zoom back out of the tip is replaced by a dissolve back to the column.
