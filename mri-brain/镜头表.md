# 镜头表 · Shot list — HOW MRI MACHINES WORK

Silent 8-bit pixel-art explainer, 1080×1920 vertical, 60 fps, exactly 70 s. Music grid 120 BPM: 1 beat = 0.5 s,
1 bar = 2 s, 35 bars. Every scene boundary of the brief (0, 3, 8, 14, 20, 28, 35, 42, 49, 58, 65, 70 s) falls on a
beat; the main climax lands on the 3D reconstruction (58–65 s).

One connected journey, there and back: MONITOR (axial MRI slice) → 3D BRAIN (the slices assemble) → the brain's
midsagittal cut = a sagittal MRI slice → the patient's head → the cutaway scanner (magnet, gradient coils, RF coil,
bore, B0 along the bore) → brain tissue → water → H₂O → one proton and its magnetic moment → a population of moments
in B0 (tiny excess = net magnetization M, precession at ω = γB0) → the RF coil and its pulse → M tipped 90° and
precessing → T1 recovery / T2 decay → gradients (slice, phase, frequency) → receiver coil → amplifier → signal
processor → ADC → k-space → inverse Fourier transform → slices → the slices stacked in 3D, segmented → the voxel brain
→ back out to the monitor, folding into the opening frame (loop).

Everything visible is computed from one model: a procedural head and brain (two hemispheres with a longitudinal
fissure, cortical folds, Sylvian and central sulci, cerebellum with folia, brainstem, ventricles, deep gray nuclei,
skull and scalp). The 3D brain is that volume rendered voxel by voxel; every MRI slice is that volume sampled with a
T1-weighted contrast; the k-space data is the real 2D Fourier transform of the slice it reconstructs.

Physics fixed for the whole film: 1.5 T superconducting scanner, B0 along the bore and always on, protons
(γ/2π = 42.58 MHz/T → 64 MHz), excess ≈ 5 per million, 90° excitation, tissue T1/T2 at 1.5 T (white matter
T1 ≈ 0.6 s, gray matter ≈ 1.0 s, CSF ≈ 4 s), slice / phase / frequency encoding, receiver coil → amplifier →
processing → ADC, Cartesian k-space, inverse 2D FFT. The 3D brain is shown as what it is: a computer reconstruction.

画风：8-bit / 16-bit 像素画（270×480 低分辨率画、4 倍最近邻放大、全片一套限色调色板＋4×4 有序抖动），Kurzgesagt 式的扁平分面、硬边发光、剖面揭开、对数尺度推拉与多层视差；脑模型是体素 2.5D：一片片切面叠成体积，再按体素逐点渲染（柔粉、灰紫、浅灰）
色板：#0b1426 #c8ced6 #22c8e8 #ffd24a #8f4cf0 #3ee070
角色：无
