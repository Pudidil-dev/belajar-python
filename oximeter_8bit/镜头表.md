## Notes

- **Locked timing:** 33 bars of 127.74 BPM = 62.000 s. Key hits sit on downbeats: the clip snap (bar 1), SpO₂ 98
  (bar 2), the dive into the fingertip (bar 3), the raw signal drop (bar 17, 30.06 s), the estimate (bar 25, 45.09 s),
  the final display (bar 28, 50.73 s, after a one-beat pull-back), the recap (bar 29), the loop (bar 33).
- **Orientation:** the hand is seen from the thumb side with the index finger pointing up; the nail faces right. The
  clip's upper jaw (display, LEDs) sits on the nail side, the lower jaw (photodiode) on the pad side, the hinge above
  the fingertip. The display is drawn on the jaw's visible face, as in the usual pulse-oximeter icon. The cut-away keeps
  the same orientation, so the light always travels right to left: LED → nail → nail bed → bone → pad (artery, vein) →
  skin → photodiode.
- **Style:** flat 2-D pixel art only (no perspective meshes), after the user's feedback on the earlier 3-D scanner.
- **Camera:** after the snap the hook settles upward toward the display (a slow tilt, not a push); holds while a mechanism plays; every dive is answered by a pull-out (5, 10, 17, 20, 24); accents only on
  the snap, the dive into the finger, the dive into blood and the signal drop.
- **Text:** one short caption at a time, top-left; short leader-line tags next to the thing they name; one disclaimer
  chip at a time (infrared shown in pink, slowed down, not to scale, pulse exaggerated, simplified).
- **Data:** the waveform comes from one pulse model (systolic upstroke, dicrotic notch) locked to the beat grid; the
  red and infrared modulation depths give R = (AC/DC)red ÷ (AC/DC)IR = 0.48, which the usual empirical line
  (SpO₂ ≈ 110 − 25 R) turns into 98%. The absorption bars use tabulated hemoglobin extinction coefficients at 660 and
  940 nm. All numbers on screen come from that model.
