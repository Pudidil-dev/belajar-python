# 镜头表 · What One Drop of Blood Looks Like Under a Microscope

36 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM, 18 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2 s; every beat on the 8th-note grid). No narration. One drop of blood, one slide, one microscope, start to finish. Short context captions at the top of the frame (≤8 words each), as the client asked in revision 2. The last frame is the eyepiece circle shrunk to the opening drop's size and place, so the video loops on a match cut.

画风：16-bit pixel-art lab game: every frame drawn on a 270×480 canvas and scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; chunky silhouettes, 1-px dark outlines, 4–8 tone shading ramps with Bayer dithering, a 5×7 pixel font for captions. The wide view is a white microscope in three-quarter view, pre-rendered like 16-bit game sprites; the x-ray view is the same model seen through, on a blueprint grid; the macro is the glass slide; the microscopic view is a stained smear through a round eyepiece. Each is its own artwork.
色板：#0D1B2A #EEF3F6 #5CC8E0 #C8202F #E48D9C #8A3580
角色：无

色板 = the repo's `navy` set (flat dark navy, ink white, cyan accent for glass highlights and the x-ray lines) plus blood red, the stain's warm pink and a red-leaning plum (hue ~307°, outside the blue-purple band). The microscope is white with dark knobs and stage, chrome objectives with coloured bands, a warm lamp.
