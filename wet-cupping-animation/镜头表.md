# 镜头表 · How Wet Cupping Works

26 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM, 13 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2 s; the main hits sit on the beat grid). One back, one main cup, start to finish. No narration, no subtitles; only a few tiny context labels (5×7 pixel font, white with a dark outline) that sit next to the thing they name. The camera is one continuous move: top view, a tilt into a side cutaway through the main cup, microscopic zooms, back up to an angled top view, and down the spine to a second clean set of spots framed exactly like the opening, so the last frame flows into the first. The film never says cupping removes toxins or cures anything; it shows suction, superficial bleeding and collection.

画风：2D 8-bit pixel art for a friendly medical simulation: a 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; chunky pixels, stepped 1-px outlines, a limited flat palette with at most a checker dither, small pixel glints on the glass. The back is a slab seen by a 2.5D camera that can tip from top-down to side-on; a cut plane through the main cup shows the skin layers. Layers and cells are drawn larger than life (schematic) and redrawn at every zoom. No 3D models, no soft gradients, no realistic gore.
色板：#0E1B30 #CF865F #C81E2B #DFE9F4 #2FA59A #A3375A
角色：无

色板 = dark navy background, warm skin, a clean friendly red for blood, pale glass with white glints, teal nitrile gloves, a reddish-purple cupping mark. Supporting tones: pink dermis, pale yellow fat, red-brown muscle, grey steel and pump, white gauze.
