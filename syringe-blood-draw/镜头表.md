# 镜头表 · How a Syringe Draws Blood

24 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM (one bar = 2 s; every beat on the 8th-note grid). No narration, no subtitles, no title card, no text on screen. One syringe, one arm, side view only. 0–20 s is the film; 20–24 s rewinds it at about 5× and lands on the opening composition, so the video loops.

画风：16-bit pixel-art retro science game: every frame drawn on a 270×480 canvas and scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; bold silhouettes, 1-px dark outlines, 3–8 tone shading ramps, Bayer dithering; the camera tracks, zooms and pulls back on the big pixels.
色板：#0D1B2A #16293D #EEF3F6 #5CC8E0 #C8202F #D9A07A
角色：无

色板 = the repo's `navy` set (flat dark navy, surface, ink white, cyan accent) plus blood red and one skin tone. The navy stays flat (hue ~210°); no blue-purple anywhere. The syringe is clear plastic with a black rubber stopper and a green-hub steel needle; the arm is skin drawn cell by cell over soft tissue with fat cells over a thick vein of flowing red cells.
