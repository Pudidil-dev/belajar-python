# How a Pregnancy Test Creates Two Lines — from hCG molecules to gold nanoparticles — shot list (镜头表)

Project: 9:16, pixel canvas 180×320 upscaled exactly 6× nearest-neighbour to 1080×1920, 60 fps, exactly 72.000 s =
4,320 unique frames (f = 0…4319 at t = f/60; a true loop: frame 4319 is the moment just before frame 0), 18 scenes of
4 s in six acts (1 the mystery 0–8, 2 inside the strip 8–20, 3 target and label 20–32, 4 capture 32–40, 5 why gold is
red 40–52, 6 control, results and loop 52–72).
Rendering: original 16-bit pixel art drawn in code by a small software renderer (Node, no browser): every shape is
rasterised on the 180×320 grid at whatever camera zoom (no bitmap scaling except the Mode-7-style rotation during the
two orientation turns), so pixels stay square and the same size in every shot.
One example is followed all the way: one hCG molecule and one gold-antibody conjugate, from the sample well to the
test line; then one spare conjugate to the control line.
Assay model (one representative architecture, not every product): mouse anti-hCG detection antibodies on colloidal
gold (cyan Y on a ruby disk), immobilised capture anti-hCG at T (mint Y), immobilised anti-mouse IgG at C (violet Y).
Device scale: flow runs upward (sample well at the bottom, C above T). Molecular scale: the view turns 90° on screen
(the strip visibly rotates, a flow arrow and a strip mini-map stay on screen) so flow runs left → right.
Music grid: 120 BPM, 4/4, 36 bars of 2 s; every event and SFX sits on the eighth-note grid (0.25 s = 15 frames).
Text: one dominant caption at a time (top, game-style dialog box, typed), short annotations beside objects, a
"Schematic • not to scale" chip during magnification. English, no narration, no subtitles.
Grammar: y1 Kurzgesagt — every idea is a concrete object, scale travel by pushing through an object into the next
world, the subject settles while the environment keeps drifting; game feel from the UI (lock-on bracket, mini-map,
dialog box) and from the chiptune score.

Notes kept with the table (they bind every shot):
- **Science:** binding happens by flow, small random motion, encounter, orientation and contact — no attraction from a
  distance; the three objects (gold, antibody, hCG) stay separate sprites; the gold particles are ruby before the test
  is used and never change colour; captured labels stay discrete dots, a line appears only because many accumulate;
  the electron cloud moves as one and never orbits or leaves the particle; colour comes from green being absorbed and
  scattered, the rest reaching the eye — no light is converted, nothing glows; the control antibody recognises the
  detection antibody, never hCG; the negative example is "hCG not detected" (an early sample can be below detection);
  no waiting time, threshold or volume is given; time is compressed and the molecular motion slowed.
- **Orientation:** sample → conjugate pad → T → C → absorbent pad everywhere; at device scale flow runs upward and C
  sits above T; at molecular scale the screen turns 90° in view of the audience and a flow arrow plus a strip mini-map
  (sample on the left, absorbent pad on the right) stay on screen.
- **The loop:** frame 0 and the last frame show the same finished cassette, framing, line intensity, background and
  question; the opening push starts from rest and the closing slide ends at rest; ambient specks drift on 72-second
  cycles; the score's bar 36 leads into bar 1 and every echo tail wraps.
