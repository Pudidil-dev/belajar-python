## Notes

- **One chain, connected:** hydrogen → proton → RF electric kicks → magnetic bending → extraction → degrader →
  energy selector → quadrupoles → dipoles → gantry → scanning nozzle → tissue → Bragg peak → DNA damage → the planned
  dose; every step is joined by a camera move or a zoom crossfade.
- **Physics honesty on screen:** the RF field gives the energy (the energy bar steps up only at gap crossings); the
  magnets only steer and focus; the degrader only slows; each quadrupole focuses one plane and defocuses the other;
  the Bragg peak is the dose of many protons, not a burst; one peak cannot cover a tumour — energies and scan spots
  together build the planned volume; the effects on cells develop over time and normal tissue is not immune.
- **Honesty chips:** BEAM INVISIBLE IN REALITY, TIME COMPRESSED, NOT TO SCALE, PLANNED IN ADVANCE, ILLUSTRATIVE VALUES,
  EFFECTS DEVELOP OVER TIME.
- **Text:** one short caption at a time in a solid box, small leader-line tags on the things they name, one honesty
  chip at a time; captions and chips leave after about 3 s.
- **Colour code:** machine white, silver and dark grey; protons luminous cyan; electrons small yellow; RF field
  alternating cyan arrows; magnetic field thin purple lines; tumour orange-red; normal tissue soft red and pink;
  deposited energy orange-yellow markers; ground deep navy.
- **Loop:** the final pull-back ends on the opening gantry composition; the first frame follows.
