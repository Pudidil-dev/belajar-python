# What was done with each review finding

The review is `审片.md`, written by an independent agent from the first full render (`film_v1.mp4`). This file records, for each of its findings, what changed in the revised film (`film_v3.mp4`), or why nothing did. Times are film seconds.

## Must fix

**M1. The gel layer showed an air band that never closed.** Fixed; it was a drawing bug.
- **Cause.** The gel shape ran along the probe face, then back along the skin, then jumped to its front edge. That made the polygon self-intersect, so only the hollows of the skin were filled. A dark band stayed between the probe face and the skin even "with gel", and pulses were drawn crossing it.
- **Gel layer.** The gel is now one continuous layer from the probe face down to the skin, in every scene that has gel (3, 4 after 16.9 s, and 6).
- **Air.** Air appears only in the "WITHOUT GEL" demonstration (14.0–16.25 s). There a real gap separates the face from the rough skin, and the pulse bounces back.
- **Gel arriving.** From 16.25 s the gel visibly flows in from the left and pushes the air out, labelled "COUPLING GEL / FILLS THE AIR GAPS". The next pulse (17.0 s) goes into the skin.

**M2. The final summary and title could not be read in time.** Fixed by giving the ending more time and room.
- **More time.** The image build now completes at 45.6 s, and the camera pulls back to the whole monitor by 46.0 s.
- **The chain.** It starts at 46.0 s, one step per 8th note, with no typing: each step appears whole, with an icon. All six steps are on screen from 47.25 s and stay until 49.3 s, about 3.3 s in all.
- **The title.** It appears at 47.0 s on the final hit and stays until 49.3 s, about 2.3 s for 28 characters.
- **Placement.** The title sits above the monitor and the chain below it (y ≤ 378 of 480, inside the reviewer's safe zone).
- **Loop.** From 49.35 s the camera pushes back into the opening shot, landing on it at 50.0 s.

**M3. Far too much text.** Fixed.
- **Removed:**
  - "SENDS SOUND, HEARS ECHOES"
  - "ELECTRICITY IN, VIBRATION OUT"
  - all three step lists
  - "ALMOST NOTHING GETS IN" and "SOUND GETS IN"
  - "ECHOES BECOME VOLTAGE BURSTS"
  - "LINE BY LINE, 30 TIMES A SECOND"
  - the "REFLECTED" header
  - the skin "0.4%" value
- **Required labels** are attached to the things they name: crystals, wire, gel, echo front, trace.
- **Caption blocks** with a context sentence remain only where the brief gives one (scenes 4, 5 and 7), plus the opening "ULTRASOUND".
- **Count.** `tools/caption_audit.py` now counts 60 text items (73 before). The character count roughly halved.
- **Reading time.** Every item meets the reviewer's rule: 17 characters/s from first appearance plus 0.3 s, at least 0.8 s.
- **Short pulse.** "SHORT PULSE: 2-3 CYCLES" now stays at a fixed position while its leader follows the pulse.
- **Rules 1 and 10.** "SOUND, NOT RADIATION" became "1540 m/s · NOT X-RAYS" and holds about 1.8 s.

**M4. Text collided with text and picture.** Fixed.
- **Scene 5 viewport.** The column is drawn in a viewport below the caption band, so the probe and the column never pass under the caption.
- **Scene 5 values.** The skin/fat values no longer stack, because the probe's own contact echo (0.4%) is no longer labelled.
- **Scene 7.** The "IMAGE RECONSTRUCTION" caption clears at 44.1 s. The camera holds until 43.9 s and pulls back only afterwards, so the monitor's readouts never pass under it.
- **Opening.** The label leaves as the punch-in starts (2.07 s), not over the zoomed speckle.
- **Scene 3.** The step lists that hid the wavefront are gone.
- **Check.** `tools/text_check.py` reports no overlaps and no text outside the frame.

**M5. "SHADOW" on the fetal brain contradicted the image.** Fixed; I agree.
- **New label.** "WEAKER BEHIND BONE" replaces "SHADOW". The brain stays fully drawn, and echoes inside it are still shown.
- **What the pulse shows.** It dims after the skull, because about a third of the energy reflects at each bone surface and the rest is partly absorbed. It does not vanish.
- **Bars.** They still show the inner skull surface returning a much weaker echo than the outer one.

**M6. Dead middle section; about 10.8 s near-still.** Fixed. Measured on `film_v2`, near-still footage dropped from about 10.8 s to 1.8 s. `film_v3` closes the 38.3–39.4 s gap; the one remaining run is 0.7 s at 6.5 s.
- **Receiver.**
  - It slides up into view as the camera rises along the wires.
  - Each echo burst arrives down the wire as a voltage packet, with a chirp, and the trace builds over 3.15 s.
  - The labels pop as the cursor reaches them.
- **Time of flight.**
  - A cursor runs down to the skull echo with a microsecond counter, then "→5.4 cm".
  - A second pass fills the gray intensity column.
  - The column then flies into the fan (see S7).
- **Echo column.** The hold now ends with a push into the probe face (29.6–31.0 s), which cuts into the listening crystals.
- **Probe shot.** The scan line sweeps through the cutaway in step with the monitor's refresh, and a signal blip runs up the cable on each sweep.

## Should fix

**S1. The echo scene was an abstract bar.** Fixed.
- **Real anatomy.** The column is now the real slice along scan line 28, taken from the same model as the image and turned to run straight down. It contains the slanted uterine wall, the fetal face in the fluid, the skull ring and the brain.
- **Direction.** Pulse fronts carry chevrons: down for the pulse, up for echoes.
- **The split.** At each boundary the echo leaves upward while the pulse continues, dimmer.
- **Strength.** Bars give echo strength and the labels give the reflected share.

**S2. Slide-show template.** Mostly fixed.
- **Text.** The repeated header block is gone from scenes 2, 3, 4 (gel part) and 6, and the step lists are gone.
- **Framing:**
  - scene 3: straight on, crystals mid-frame;
  - gel demonstration: a 1.5× macro of the face and the skin;
  - scene 6: further back, crystals near the top and the echoes rising from below.
- **Transitions:**
  - 14.0 and 22.0 s are hard cuts on the beat; 22.0 s is matched on the descending pulse front;
  - 31.0 s is a push-through cut;
  - 35 s is a slide-up;
  - 42 s is the flying scan line.
- **Remaining dissolves.** One stays for the scale change into the probe (8.4 s), and one under the flying column (42 s).

**S3. Soft, blurry hold after the opening zoom.** Fixed.
- **Crisp cells.** At high zoom the image is drawn from the nearest scan line and sample. It shows as crisp cells of the polar grid: an image made of scan lines, not a photograph.
- **Shorter hold.** The hold is about 0.6 s.
- **Pull-back.** It starts at 3.0 s with a fast-start ease, on the drop.

**S4. Audio: accent before the reveal, bass-heavy mix.** Addressed. I still could not listen to it.
- **The reveal.** It now moves from 3.0 s, on the impact, and a whoosh cue sits on the pull-back.
- **Bass and saturation.** The bass bus is saturated (upper harmonics), and the band below ~140 Hz is cut by about 2–6 dB.
- **Click layer.** A short 3 kHz click layer sits on every kick.
- **Energy below 120 Hz:** 54%, down from about 63%.
- **Through a 300 Hz high-pass:** −17.0 LUFS, up from −18.0, against −14.3 integrated.
- **Not done:** cutting further would thin the beat on headphones. Not checked on a real phone.

**S5. Deformation too small; one firing; no send/listen alternation.** Mostly fixed.
- **Deformation.** It is drawn about 2.2× larger (the label says "SLOW MOTION, NOT TO SCALE").
- **Cycle bar.** Scene 6 has a SEND / LISTEN bar ("1 µs", "156 µs") with a cursor. SEND lights at the start and again when the listening window ends, and a new pulse fires.
- **Not done:** several transmit pulses in scene 3. In the slowed-down picture, a second pulse 0.5 s later would be fired long before the first pulse's echoes return. Real machines wait the ~156 µs round trip, which is what the cycle bar shows. The crystal's 2–3 ringing cycles give the rhythmic clicks instead.

**S6. The pressure wave read as light beams.** Fixed.
- **Particles.** In tissue the bright crest lines are no longer drawn. The particles themselves carry the wave: displacement up to about 5.5 px, compressed rows turn white/yellow, and stretched rows go darker.
- **Labels.** The "COMPRESSION" leader snaps to the first compression crest, and the pressure curve stays on the right.

**S7. Signal → image was a dissolve.** Fixed.
- **The mapping.** The gray intensity column flies from beside the trace into its scan line in the fan (41.6–42.3 s), narrowing as it goes.
- **The build.** The other 95 lines then fill in sweep order, accelerating, with a tick per line pair.

**S8. Key text in the platform UI zone.** Fixed.
- Essential text is kept above y ≈ 378 of 480: the chain, the scene notes and the line counter, which now sits in the caption block.
- The pressure curve and right-hand panels end by x ≈ 240.

**S9. The hero image read as "an ultrasound", not "a baby".** Improved.
- The face bumps (forehead, nose, lips, chin) are larger.
- The arm now rises only in front of the chest and chin, so it no longer covers the profile.
- The pose matches in the cutaway.
