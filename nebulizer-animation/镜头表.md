# 镜头表 · How a Nebulizer Turns Liquid Into Mist

20 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM (one bar = 2 s, ten bars = the whole loop; every hit sits on the 8th-note
grid). No title card, no narration.

The film is one continuous camera move along the aerosol path: person → mask → connector → outlet rim → cutaway →
one mesh hole → three holes → back out with one droplet → person. The last frame lands on the first frame.

Revision 2 (client: "more context and explanation"): an explanation layer in the film's own game style.
- **Captions.** An RPG-style caption box at the top types one short line per step.
- **Part tags.** Small name tags pin the parts while each shot holds.
- **HUD.** A plate at the top right shows the zoom factor, a scale bar (1 cm … 5 µm) and the slow-motion factor (up to
  195,000×).

English by default, Bahasa Indonesia with `--lang id`. `--clean` renders the minimal cut of revision 1 (only the
"VIBRATING MESH" tag).

画风：16-bit pixel-art game (SNES era) with chunky 8-bit silhouettes: every frame drawn on a 270×480 canvas and scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; the world is re-rasterized at every zoom level (mm → µm), so zooming in reveals new detail instead of stretching a sprite; 3–6 tone ramps, Bayer dither, 1-px outlines, flat dark navy background; captions and tags in the same 5×7 bitmap font inside an SNES-style dialog box.
色板：navy（#0D1B2A #16293D #EEF3F6 #8FA3B3 #5CC8E0 #F2A93B）
角色：一位成年患者的侧脸（像素画，写实比例，有脖子、肩、手腕和握持的手；过 references/角色精细度.md 交付线八条，客户 brief 指定开场必须是侧脸戴面罩吸入）

色板 = the skill's `navy` set (flat navy #0D1B2A, hue ≈ 211°, outside the blue-purple zone). Device = light gray
(the brief), liquid medicine and mist = the cyan accent, piezo ceramic and drive glow = the amber accent, skin and
hair warm ramps. Caption keywords use the same two accents: cyan for the liquid, mist and droplets, amber for the
piezo and the numbers.

## 镜头表 lint 黄灯说明

- **⑥ Short shots** (1.2–1.9 s). The film is one continuous camera move, so the rows here are the steps of the
  explanation layer, not cuts. Each caption holds about 1.7–2.3 s and is fully typed at least 0.7 s before it closes.
- **④ Text in the first shot.** The opening caption runs across the loop point (18.9 s → 1.2 s), so frame 0 and the
  last frame stay identical and the loop is not noticed. The picture still opens on the patient inhaling: the
  caption is a small box at the top, not a title card.
- **⑪ Numbers in a shot.** The numbers (≈5 µm, 130,000×, 195,000×) are written in the caption box over the moving
  scene, never as a separate number card. They are the real figures for a vibrating-mesh nebulizer: droplets 1–5 µm,
  ~130 kHz drive. 195,000× is the film's own slow motion: one 7.7 µs cycle shown as 1.5 s.
- **Gate yellow: fixed frame 77%** (`film_gate.py`, half-size QA preview). The caption box and the HUD plate stay
  at the top while the picture moves underneath. This is the explanation layer the client asked for: the box opens and
  closes with each step and is never a page frame around a still picture. The `--clean` cut has none of it.
