## 制作说明（制作者填写；只写在这一段，事实段不要动）

- 用了哪个语法 / 风格：
  - **Style.** The y1 Kurzgesagt grammar drawn in 8-bit pixels, on the same 14_8bit pipeline as the earlier films in this
    repo:
    - every frame is drawn at 270×480 with one palette, 4×4 Bayer dither and a 4× nearest-neighbour upscale (60 fps);
    - the colour code is the brief's: cyan electrons, purple quantum states, blue superconducting circuits, gold
      hardware, orange microwave pulses, green classical results and red noise, on a near-black background.
  - **One journey.** Lab → refrigerator → processor → aluminium → Josephson junction → transmon → Bloch sphere → two
    qubits → readout chain → error-correcting patch → back out to the lab.
    - Each scene's camera starts where the previous one stopped, and each scene hands over its last overlay and
      hardware look (`S07.overlay`, `S08.decor`, `S10.markers` …) so the next one can slide or fade it out.
    - There is one planned hard cut, at 17 s, into the refrigerator.
  - **Physics drives the graphics:**
    - state vectors with gates as continuous fractional powers (H^s, CNOT(s)), partial traces for the two-qubit
      reduced vectors, and phasor sums in the interference diagram;
    - a split-step Schrödinger solution for tunnelling;
    - U = −EJ cos φ with transmon levels for EJ/EC = 30 (5.0 / 4.7 GHz);
    - real hardware numbers (Tc 1.2 K, the plate temperatures, ≈1.5 nm AlOx, 7 GHz readout, ≈100 µs coherence).
  - **Shot list.** `镜头表.md`: 27 shots, lint green. No three-direction check was made, because the style direction
    is the one already used for the previous films in this repo; only the subject is new.

- 「六种做坏方式」逐条自查：
  1. **Slideshow look.** No page frames and no bullet lists. Every scene shows a process while it happens, and the
     camera moves vary: dive, vertical track, 8-decade push, glide, magnifier pull-out, pan along the drive line, a
     push into the Bloch sphere with a close orbit, orbits between gates, overlay hand-overs, a scroll up the readout
     chain, a push on the code patch, and the final pull-out. The ending is the machine in the lab, with the title and
     the chain above and below it.
  2. **Copying the demos.** Nothing is taken from the skill's demos or sample specs. Only `util.js`, `motion.js` and
     `camera.js` (MIT) come from the skill engine, and the pixel pipeline from this repo's earlier films.
  3. **No concrete object.** Every shot has named objects doing something: the refrigerator, plates, cables and
     attenuators, the transistor, the chip and its transmon crosses, the junction leads and AlOx barrier, Al ions and
     electrons, drive lines and pulses, readout resonators, amplifiers and the digitizer, IQ clouds, the histogram,
     and the surface-code patch.
  4. **Dead backgrounds and empty frames.** Backgrounds are near-black by design (the brief asks for dark backgrounds).
     The gate finds no blank stretch, the dead-background share is 0.020, and the longest still stretch is 2.0 s.
  5. **Drawing what could be footage.** Not applicable: none of this can be filmed, and the brief asks for a drawn
     pixel-art animation.
  6. **AI look.** Purple appears only on quantum states and blue only on superconducting circuits, both requested by
     the user. The blue/purple background share is 0.075 (green even without the declaration). One style and one
     palette run throughout, with no characters and no faces.
- 黄灯项逐条说明（时间码＋为什么可以这样）：
  - 48–78s 这段几乎没有快动作（前紧后松）：This is the quantum-state part: the transmon's levels and pulse, superposition,
    and the start of interference.
    - The shot list holds the camera here (停 / 缓推) so the viewer can read the Bloch sphere, the amplitude columns,
      the phase dials and the equations, following "看得清就不动".
    - The picture is never static. The longest still stretch is 2.0 s (green), and at every moment something
      happens: the pulse travels down the line, rings burst at the drop, the vector turns about the H axis, the
      camera pushes into the sphere and orbits it once (60.0–64.6 s), a crossed-out "fast switch" card flickers, the
      sphere orbits to the Z gate's view, the Z gate swings the vector, and a second pulse arrives.
    - The gate's fast-motion measure needs a large bright area moving. These are small graphics on a dark background,
      so they do not register.
    - The music carries the energy: drop 1 lands at 55 s, on the pulse.
  - 最长 37.3s 没有任何切换或快动作：This is the same stretch (about 48–85 s), up to the camera swing and overlay hand-over
    at the second drop (84 s). The reasons are the same as above. Between those points the scene changes at 55 s and
    70 s are continuous hand-overs rather than cuts, by design.

- **Accuracy notes for the viewer.**
  - Gates (tens of ns) and readout (≈1 µs) are slowed about 10⁸ times.
  - Atoms and the barrier are magnified.
  - On-chip pulses are drawn as travelling packets; a real pulse is longer than the chip.
  - The wave packet is the textbook single-particle picture of tunnelling, and the junction is then shown as two
    coherent condensates with phases φ₁, φ₂.
  - Measurement counts and the surface-code errors are illustrative. See `README.md`.
