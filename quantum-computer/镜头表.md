# 镜头表 · Shot list — HOW QUANTUM COMPUTERS WORK

Silent 8-bit pixel-art explainer, 1080×1920 vertical, 60 fps, exactly 120 s. Music grid 120 BPM: 1 beat = 0.5 s,
1 bar = 2 s, 60 bars. Every scene boundary of the brief (0, 7, 17, 30, 44, 55, 70, 84, 99, 109, 116, 120 s) falls on a
beat; drops land on superposition (55 s) and entanglement (84 s).

One continuous journey through a real superconducting quantum computer: LAB → GOLDEN DILUTION REFRIGERATOR → COOLING
STAGES → PROCESSOR → QUBIT → ALUMINIUM FILM (Cooper-pair condensate) → JOSEPHSON JUNCTION → TRANSMON → MICROWAVE CONTROL
→ BLOCH SPHERE (H, Z, H) → TWO QUBITS (Bell state) → READOUT RESONATOR + AMPLIFIERS → CLASSICAL BITS → NOISE + ERROR
CORRECTION → back out to the lab.

The quantum graphics are driven by the actual state vector: every gate is applied as a matrix to the amplitudes, and
the Bloch vector, the amplitude bars, the phase arrows and the joint two-qubit bars are drawn from those numbers.
Labels are minimal: the brief's scene labels, kets and equations, temperatures, and small part names.

Physics fixed for the whole film: a fixed-frequency transmon (Al/AlOx/Al junction shunted by a capacitor, f01 ≈ 5 GHz,
anharmonicity ≈ −0.3 GHz), dispersive readout through a resonator (≈ 7 GHz), ideal gates in scenes 6–9 (labelled IDEAL),
real noise only in scene 10.

画风：8-bit / 16-bit 像素画（270×480 低分辨率画、4 倍最近邻放大、全片一套限色调色板＋4×4 有序抖动），用 Kurzgesagt 的扁平分面、发光但硬边的粒子与波、剖面揭开和对数尺度推拉；近黑底，金色量子硬件，青色电子，紫色量子态，蓝色超导电路
色板：#05060c #ffcc4a #3fe4ff #9b5cff #3c8cff #ff8a2a
角色：无
允许蓝紫：用户点名「Purple quantum states」「Blue superconducting circuits」；紫色只画量子态，蓝色只画超导电路，底色仍是近黑
