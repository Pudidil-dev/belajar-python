# 镜头表 · How Blood Centrifuges Separate Blood

28 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 120 BPM (one bar = 2 s, every cut and hit on the 8th-note grid). No narration, no subtitles.

Revision 2 (client request): an animated cutaway of the centrifuge spinning was added (shots 4, 5 and 8). The casing is sliced open to show the motor, the drive shaft and the swing-out rotor, whose buckets swing out level as it spins. The film grew from 24 s to 28 s.

画风：16-bit pixel-art game (SNES era): every frame drawn on a 270×480 canvas and scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; 3–5 tone shading ramps, 1-px dark outlines, Bayer dithering for gradients, hard-edged sprite particles; zooms are done on the big pixels (camera scales the pixel grid), never blurred.
色板：#121417 #1E2226 #F2EFE8 #C8202F #F2B33D #5FB3A1
角色：无

色板 = the repo's `ink` set (charcoal bg, surface, warm white, gold, teal) plus blood red. Machine = warm white with teal-grey shadows; tube cap = teal (lithium-heparin tube, a real plasma tube); plasma = gold; cells = red. No blue-purple anywhere.
