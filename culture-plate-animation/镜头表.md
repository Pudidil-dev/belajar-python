# 镜头表 · How Bacteria Grow on a Culture Plate

30 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM, 15 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2 s; one beat = exactly 15 frames). Revision 2 (client request): redrawn in the blood centrifuge film's 16-bit style, the repo's best-performing video. One culture plate on a clean, dark lab bench, seen from straight above, from a blank plate to a white zigzag of colonies, then on to the next plate. A white cotton swab streaks a zigzag across the agar; an RPG target cursor locks onto one point of the streak and the camera dives into it through a round lens. Down there one rod-shaped bacterium grows, pinches in the middle and splits in two (pop), then the two split, then four, faster and faster, while the camera steps back after every round and more bacteria along the streak do the same, until they merge into one white crowd. The lens closes, the camera pulls out to the whole plate, and in a time-lapse (a pixel clock spins) white colonies rise along the zigzag, thick where the swab started and as separate dots where it ended. A shine runs along the finished pattern; the camera slides along the bench to the next fresh plate, which is the first frame. No narration, no subtitles, no text at all.

画风：16-bit pixel-art game (SNES era), the same look as the repo's blood centrifuge film: top-down over a dark charcoal lab bench (black epoxy) with a soft pool of lamp light, so the pale plate glows; every frame drawn on a 270×480 canvas and scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; 5–9 tone ramps shaded with ordered (Bayer) dithering, 1-px dark outlines, rim-lit glass, hard-edged sprite particles (sparkles, bursts, ring waves, gold light rays); everything redrawn at the camera's own scale; a light game layer with no text (a gold target cursor, a lens with reticle ticks, a division progress bar, a pixel alarm clock).
色板：#121417 #F1DDA6 #FFFEF8 #ADC6C0 #F2B33D #5FB3A1
角色：无（no people and no faces: the swab is held by an off-screen hand; the bacteria are plain rod-shaped cells）

色板 = the repo's `ink` set (charcoal bench, teal-grey glass, gold, teal) plus pale yellow agar and white-cream colonies. Supporting tones: deep amber where the agar climbs the dish wall, warm white for the swab and the clock, brushed aluminium for the lens barrel, cream rods with a warm dark outline up close. No blue-purple anywhere.
