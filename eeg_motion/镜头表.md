## Notes

- **One causal chain:** synaptic input → transmembrane ionic currents → postsynaptic potentials → summed
  extracellular fields of aligned, synchronous pyramidal neurons → volume conduction through brain, CSF, skull and
  scalp → scalp electrodes (with gel) → differential voltage measurement → differential amplifier → common-mode
  rejection (not perfect) → filtering → analog-to-digital sampling → the EEG waveform.
- **Kept honest without words:** EEG records potential differences and never injects current; single action
  potentials are not the main source; the skull is not an insulator; fields are explanatory graphics, not light;
  the amplifier and filters create no neural signal; noise rejection and filtering are imperfect; eyes-closed
  posterior alpha is normal and blocks when the eyes open; not every channel looks the same; EEG is not imaging and
  does not read thoughts; time is compressed.
- **The loop:** the O1 trace at frame 0 is the same trace the camera settles on at 55 s; the waveform is one
  signal sampled continuously through the boundary (every component completes whole cycles in 55 s), the scroll
  speed and direction never change, the camera comes to rest on the opening composition and leaves it from rest.
- **Mix low** for a later voiceover; exports: EEG_How_It_Works (music + SFX), _No_Music, _No_SFX, _Silent, stems.
