## Notes

- **Locked timing:** 65 bars of 120 BPM = 130.000 s. The hook packs the brief's five beats into 4 s (curve, dive,
  approach, cut, glow); after it the pace slows: every enzyme interaction plays approach → binding → reaction →
  product → transition before the camera moves on.
- **Orientation:** strands are drawn horizontally. Every template is shown with its 3' end on the left, so every new
  strand grows 5'→3' from left to right and every polymerase travels to the right; the probe always sits to the right
  of the primer it will be cut by. After the RNA–cDNA hybrid parts, the cDNA turns over once so the convention holds.
- **Colour code:** virus cyan + muted purple, RNA magenta, cDNA electric blue (it stays blue as the template it
  becomes), other template strands deep blue, new strands warm orange, reverse transcriptase purple, DNA polymerase
  cyan/silver, primers yellow, probe light green with a neon-green reporter and a dark purple quencher, excitation
  light blue, fluorescence green, photodiode metallic cyan, electrons electric blue, signals neon green, heating
  orange, cooling blue, ground dark navy.
- **Style:** flat 2-D pixel art only (no perspective meshes), as in the previous films.
- **Camera:** scale travel joined by lens portals (dive into the next world inside a growing circle, back out in a
  shrinking one), a rewind after the hook, pans that follow one molecule, and pull-backs after every dive (4, 6, 12,
  16, 18, 20, 25). Accents only on the hook dive, the dive into reverse transcription, the dive into the active
  site, the dive into the photodiode and the result.
- **Text:** one short caption at a time, top-left; short leader-line tags next to the thing they name; one honesty
  chip at a time (TIME-LAPSE, EXAMPLE PROGRAM, NOT TO SCALE, ILLUSTRATIVE SEQUENCE, IDEALIZED DOUBLING,
  SIMPLIFIED, RNA DETECTED ≠ INFECTIOUS VIRUS).
- **Data:** one amplification model drives every curve on screen (baseline, exponential phase, plateau, threshold
  crossing at Cq 26.4); the thermal program follows a typical probe one-step protocol (55 °C RT, 95 °C, 95 ↔ 60 °C).
