# 镜头表 · How Pills Get Into Blister Packs (v2)

20 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM, 10 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2 s; every machine hit sits on the 16th-note grid). One capsule, one blister card, one machine, start to finish. Short-video structure: the payoff first (the pill bursting out of the pack, flying at the camera), the question it asks, a tape glitch back in time, five game stages (one locked shot per station, each cut on the beat), a boss warning, a final "boss", and a seamless loop: the last frame is the thumb pressing the dome, the first frame is the pop. No narration and no subtitles: one line of RPG dialogue, short stage titles, and pop-up words next to the things they name.

v2 is a redesign of v1 (the client rated v1 2/10): a top-down 3/4 view like a classic RPG map instead of a side view; the line runs from the top of the screen to the bottom, which suits 9:16; the subjects are big (a capsule is 50–200 px long instead of 20); one locked camera per stage with the web flowing through it, instead of long pans.

画风：2D 8-bit RPG pixel art seen from above at 3/4, like a top-down RPG map: a 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; chunky pixels, stepped 1-px outlines, a limited flat palette with at most a checker or ordered dither; height shows as an upward shift plus a little perspective, raised things cast a shadow down and to the right.
色板：#0F3A3C #25A879 #E2343C #FFD736 #AAB6BC #DC966B
角色：只出手（one big bare hand at the end, no face; the capsule has no face either: it is marked as the player by a yellow "YOU ▼" tag, and it speaks through an RPG text box whose name tab is a little picture of it）

色板 = deep teal factory floor, mint machine paint, the red half of the capsule, the yellow game text, the aluminium foil, the hand. Supporting tones: the capsule's white half, the dark steel bed, clear-film glints, orange for heat only, light blue for air only, a UI green for PASS and a UI crimson for MISS / REJECT / WARNING.
