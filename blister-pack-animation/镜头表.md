# 镜头表 · How Pills Get Into Blister Packs

20 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 120 BPM, 10 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2 s; every machine hit sits on the beat grid). One capsule, one blister card, one machine, start to finish. Short-video structure: the payoff first (the pill popping out of the pack), the question it asks, a rewind that previews the whole line, six game levels (one per station), a final "boss", and a seamless loop: the last frame is the thumb pressing the dome, the first frame is the pop. No narration and no subtitles: a hook question, short level titles, and small labels next to the things they name.

画风：2D 8-bit RPG pixel art, the same as the vortex-mixer film: a 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; chunky pixels, stepped 1-px outlines, a limited flat palette with at most a checker or ordered dither. The blister machine is seen from the side like a side-scrolling game level, in a pharma factory drawn in parallax layers.
色板：#1D3B34 #E8EEF0 #E2343C #FFD736 #FF8A1F #6EC6F0
角色：只出手（one big bare hand at the end, no face; the capsule has no face either: it is marked as the player by a yellow "YOU ▼" tag, like a game's player indicator）

色板 = dark pharma-green factory wall, white and steel machine, the red half of the capsule, the yellow game text, orange for heat only, light blue for air and water only. Supporting tones: the capsule's white half, aluminium greys for the foil, the hand's skin tones, a UI green for PASS and a UI crimson for REJECT.
