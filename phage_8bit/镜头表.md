# How Bacteriophages Infect Bacteria — shot list (镜头表)

Project: 9:16, logical 270×480 ×4 → 1080×1920, 60 fps, exactly 72.000 s (4,320 frames), 22 scenes, no narration.
Music grid: 130 BPM, 39 bars = 72.000 s exactly (13 beats = 6 s). Scene starts are snapped to the 8th-note grid
(≤0.12 s from the brief); every key event (lock, contraction, takeover, factory, burst) sits on a downbeat.
Skill: huashu-art-motion, open tier (own renderer). Grammar: y1 Kurzgesagt scale travel inside ONE continuous world
(the bacterium is drawn at every scale, so the camera never teleports: it pulls out, glides and dives between
the cell, the envelope, the molecules and back). Pixel pipeline: 14_8bit (low-res canvas, nearest-neighbour ×4).
One example from start to finish: the same T4 phage, the same E. coli cell, the same viral DNA; the last shot
pushes into one of its progeny, which becomes the hero of frame one (seamless loop).

## Notes

- **Locked timing:** the 22 time ranges follow the brief (snapped to the 8th-note grid). Transitions happen inside
  scenes (continuous camera), so most boundaries are not cuts.
- **Camera:** pushes alternate with holds, glides and pull-outs; the only accent move is the sheath contraction
  (快推). The burst uses a fast pull-out reveal; the end pushes into the progeny that becomes frame one.
- **Text:** one caption at a time, top-left, typed; small tags sit next to the thing they name.
- **Scale:** T4 vs E. coli is close to real (phage height ≈ 1/3 of the cell width); molecular close-ups are
  magnified in the same world. Time is compressed (a real lytic cycle takes ~25–30 min).
- **Lint (storyboard_lint):** no red. Two yellows, both from the brief: scene 01 is 1.85 s (the brief's hook is
  0–2 s, snapped to one bar) and the first shot carries six words (the brief requires "Bacteriophage / A virus that
  infects bacteria" in 0–2 s; the title waits 0.22 s so the picture speaks first).
- **film_gate on a 540×960 QA proxy (not a deliverable):** all three gates green — fast-motion frames 0.27 (needs ≥ 0.06),
  no blank frames, blue-purple frames 0.00. Fixed-frame share dropped from 0.49 to 0.08 after removing the caption
  plates. One yellow remains: ~9 push-ins per minute. They are the Kurzgesagt scale dives the topic needs (cell →
  envelope → molecule and back), and every dive is answered by a pull-out (pushes are 52% of zoom events).
- **Skipped direction gate:** the brief locks every scene (with the user's own storyboard sheet and colour guide)
  and the pixel/Kurzgesagt look is the repository's established, approved house style; no new direction was
  needed, so the three-direction gate was not run.
