## Notes

- **Locked timing:** the 26 time ranges follow the brief (snapped to the 8th-note grid).
- **Orientation:** one vertical stack for 9:16 — fingertip on top, the strip tip-up in the meter below; blood runs down
  the channel, electrons run down the electrode, data runs into the meter (the gold electrode is the left wall in the
  strip section and in the molecular close-ups).
- **Camera:** holds while a mechanism plays; pushes go into the next world and are answered by pull-outs back out;
  two accents only (the dive into the strip, the number slam).
- **Text:** one caption at a time, top-left, typed; small leader-line tags next to the thing they name.
- **Scale:** the strip cross-section and the molecules are drawn magnified and not to scale (chip on screen).
- **Science:** a generic modern electrochemical strip (an FAD glucose dehydrogenase–type enzyme, a ferricyanide-like
  mediator, a working electrode plus counter/reference and a fill-detect electrode); no brand layout.
- **Lint (storyboard_lint):** no red. Yellows, all from the brief: the first shot carries the brief's own caption
  ("One tiny drop of blood", typed after the click so the picture speaks first); scenes 02 and 04 are 1.86 s (the
  brief's 2 s beats snapped to the grid); the loop ending is 6.96 s but holds three new events (the touch replayed,
  the strip pulling away, the pen coming back to frame one).
