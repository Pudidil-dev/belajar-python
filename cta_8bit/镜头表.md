# How CT Angiography Detects a Brain Aneurysm — shot list (镜头表)

Project: 9:16, logical 180×320 ×6 → 1080×1920 (chunky 8/16-bit pixels), 60 fps, exactly 60.000 s (3,600 unique frames
0..3599 at t = f/60, a true loop: frame 3599 is the moment just before frame 0), 24 micro-scenes in 6
continuous sequences (A the hidden aneurysm 0–6, B intravenous contrast 6–20, C X-ray attenuation 20–31, D digital CT
reconstruction 31–42, E identifying the aneurysm 42–54, F the loop 54–60). NO on-screen text of any kind: no titles,
labels, numbers, symbols or UI; the explanation is carried by objects, motion, colour and sound.
Rendering: genuine 3D — a software rasterizer written for this project (perspective cameras, z-buffer, procedural
meshes for the arteries, the aneurysm, brain, head, heart, lungs, scanner) whose output is native pixel art: palette
ramps with ordered dithering, screen-door transparency, one-pixel outlines. One coherent patient frame in millimetres;
one reusable aneurysm mesh at the LEFT middle cerebral artery bifurcation, seen in every sequence, always closed
(an unruptured saccular aneurysm). The film opens already orbiting the aneurysm clockwise and ends on the same orbit at
the same angular speed (Hermite keys with matched slopes), so the loop has no fade, freeze, flash or cut.
Music grid: 100 BPM, 25 bars of 4/4 = 60.000 s; a sixteenth is 0.15 s = 9 frames, so every event sits on a frame.
Skill: huashu-art-motion, open tier (own renderer). Grammar: y1 Kurzgesagt scale travel in 3D. One example throughout:
one adult patient, one injection in the right arm, one contrast bolus, one CT scanner, one dataset, one aneurysm.

画风：3D low-poly voxel pixel art in Kurzgesagt flat-science grammar: true perspective, 3–5 step palette ramps with ordered dithering, same-hue dark outlines, screen-door translucency for cutaways, no bloom, no neon, one style in every shot

## Notes

- **One chain, connected:** arm vein → central veins → right atrium → right ventricle → pulmonary arteries → lungs →
  pulmonary veins → left atrium → left ventricle → aorta → carotid → internal carotid → MCA → X-ray attenuation →
  detector measurements → reconstruction → slices → voxel volume → vascular rendering → the aneurysm.
- **Physics honesty without words:** the contrast is an amber concentration inside the blood (never glowing, never on
  the wall, never leaving the vessels); veins and the right heart are drawn steel blue, arteries and the left heart
  red, so the order of the circulation reads by colour and motion; the X-ray tube makes the beam, iodine only absorbs
  more of it (fewer photons reach the detector behind the artery); projections are abstract patterns, never finished
  slices; the slices come from the reconstruction, the 3D vessels from the slices; nothing diagnoses itself.
- **The anchor:** one aneurysm mesh, one clockwise camera orbit — frame 3599 leads straight into frame 0 (same pose,
  same angular speed, the contrast washed out of the brain arteries again by 56.2 s, the music's bar 25 resolving into bar 1).
- **The machine:** the tube and the curved multi-row detector are mounted opposite each other on the rotor disc and turn
  together (slow motion: a real rotor turns in about 0.3 s); the collimator shapes the fan; each detector element shows
  the intensity it measures (from the projections of the same head); the table moves the head through for the helix.
- **Face never shown:** whenever the camera is near the head, the head is a see-through rim (with hair); seen whole,
  it is far away or hidden by the gantry.
- **Music low** (headroom for a later Azerbaijani voiceover); exports: full mix, no music, no SFX, silent.
