# 镜头表 · Shot list — HOW DOES A FOLEY CATHETER WORK?

*Where does this tube actually go? What keeps it there?* A 60-second vertical explainer for Reels / TikTok.
- **Format.** 1080×1920 (9:16), 60 fps, exactly 60 s: 20 connected scenes of exactly 3 s.
- **Pixels.** Drawn at 360×640 and upscaled exactly 3× with nearest-neighbour. One limited palette, ordered dithering.
- **Sound.** No narration and no dialogue. Minimal English labels drawn by the editor layer: one dominant caption at a time; the clean version (`?notext=1`) has none, and `docs/labels.srt` lists them for re-editing. A restrained 120 BPM electronic score (30 bars) and quiet synced effects leave the mid-range free for a voice-over.
- **Loop.** Scene 20 pushes into a foreground segment of the catheter until it fills the frame; scene 1 opens with the fast pull-back out of that same segment.

## The one example that runs through the film

**Anatomy.** One adult male pelvis in sagittal section (`engine/lib/pelvis.js`, millimetres, anterior to the right), shown as a 2.5D voxel diorama:
- bladder (muted-rose wall, urine inside), bladder neck, prostate (subdued purple-grey);
- the S-curved urethra: prostatic, membranous through the pelvic floor, bulbar, penile;
- pubic symphysis, rectum, a translucent neutral body shell;
- the penis in clinical cross-section only: corpora and the urethra, no external detail.

**Device.** One two-way Foley catheter (`engine/lib/foley.js`), the same in every view:
- rounded closed tip → two opposing side drainage eyes → retention balloon → shaft toward the outlet;
- one large drainage lumen and one small balloon lumen with a solid partition;
- outside the body: the main drainage connector and the separate balloon valve, the tubing, a thigh securement and a bag supported on the bed frame below bladder level.

## Fixed for the whole film

- **The route.** The catheter follows the urethral lumen into the bladder; it never enters a ureter or crosses tissue. The movement in S01–S02 is a compressed schematic overview, not a placement procedure (no forceful pushing, no syringe, no volumes).
- **The balloon.** It is only ever seen expanded inside the bladder (S03 is a cut to the completed placement state), resting above the bladder neck. It is liquid-filled, not a suction cup or a seal, and it does not guarantee against displacement. It never pumps.
- **Urine's route.** Urine is made by the kidneys and reaches the bladder by the ureters. It enters through the side eyes and runs in the drainage lumen past the balloon, never through the balloon chamber, out of the drainage port, down the closed tubing into the bag.
- **The bag.** Independently supported, below bladder level, off the floor; the tubing has gentle curves and no dependent loops.
- **Colours are overlays.** Amber (drainage path) and cyan (balloon channel) are teaching overlays, not fluid colours. Flow markers show direction only. Magnification and timing are schematic.
