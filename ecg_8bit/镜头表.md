# How ECG Machines Read Your Heart — shot list (镜头表)

Project: 9:16, logical 360×640 ×3 → 1080×1920, 60 fps, exactly 48.000 s (2,880 frames), 20 micro-scenes grouped in
6 continuous sequences (A hook 0–3, B where the signal begins 3–12, C through the heart 12–26, D how electrodes read
it 26–35, E how the machine processes it 35–43, F the result 43–48). Micro-scenes inside a sequence are joined by
camera moves and soft zoom crossfades, not cuts.
Music grid: 120 BPM, 24 bars of 4/4 = 48.000 s exactly. The heart rate on screen is 72 BPM (one beat every 5/3 of a
music beat): the real-time monitor beeps of the hook and the ending fall on the triplet grid, and the slowed beat in
the middle puts the R peak and its beep on the downbeat of bar 12 (22.0 s), the drop.
Skill: huashu-art-motion, open tier (own renderer). Grammar: y1 Kurzgesagt scale travel. One example from start to
finish: one patient, one monitor, three electrodes (RA, LA, LL), one heart, one heartbeat followed in slow motion,
one Lead II waveform, one amplifier chain.

画风：16-bit high-detail pixel art in Kurzgesagt flat-science grammar: dark navy ground, flat 2–3 tone shading with same-hue dark edges, soft smooth glows only on light sources and screens (no flashes, no lightning), one style in every shot

角色：只出手和背影 — no faces: only the patient's chest (neck to waist) with the electrodes; the monitor, the heart and the cells carry the story

## Notes

- **Sequences, not cuts:** six sequences joined by camera tracking, pushes, pull-backs and soft zoom crossfades; no
  portals, no flashes, no lightning (the brief: electrical activity is not drawn as bolts).
- **Pacing:** one heartbeat is followed in slow motion from 3 s to 26 s (a SLOWED DOWN chip says so); the ECG strip
  at the bottom keeps the same Lead II shape with real proportions while a playhead walks through it, so the P wave,
  the PR segment, the QRS and the T wave each line up with what the heart is doing.
- **Cause and effect:** ion currents change one cell's voltage → gap junctions pass activation on → the coordinated
  wave produces voltage differences across the body → the electrodes sense them → the amplifier takes the difference
  → filters → ADC → screen. No ion travels from the heart to the skin.
- **Text:** one short caption at a time in a solid high-contrast box, large leader-line tags, one honesty chip at a
  time (SLOWED DOWN, SIMPLIFIED FIELD MAP, SENSES VOLTAGE · NO SHOCK, SHARED HUM MOSTLY CANCELS, SAME SHAPE, BIGGER,
  FILTERS CAN CHANGE SHAPE, SAMPLES SHOWN SLOWED); captions and chips leave after about 3 s.
- **Colour code:** heart muscle crimson, SA node warm yellow, AV node orange, conduction pathways electric cyan, cell
  membrane deep purple-red, Na⁺ yellow, Ca²⁺ cyan, K⁺ purple, electrodes white and silver, electrical signal neon
  green, digital processing cyan, monitor dark with a green trace.
