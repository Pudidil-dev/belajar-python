# 镜头表 · The Life Cycle of a Skin Cell

35 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 96 BPM, 14 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2.5 s). One cell, one slice of skin, one camera that never cuts away. The structure comes from the client's scenario: a flake of dead skin lifts off in flowing water (the hook); the camera dives back into the skin to where it came from, down to a basal cell about to divide. The highlight is that division: the camera zooms into the basal cell, through its membrane, and watches it divide in slow motion (4 extra bars): its DNA, already copied, packs into chromosomes; a spindle catches them and lines them up; each chromosome splits and one copy goes to each end; a ring pinches the cell into two cells, one above the other. The bottom one stays on the basement membrane; the top one is the cell we follow. The camera comes back out as the new cell pops up into the layer above, and the story goes on: it is pushed up through the layers, changes, dies and becomes a flat tile; the bonds holding the top tiles break and they peel away; the camera settles back on the opening shot, where our cell is now the flake about to lift off, so the last frame runs into the first. No narration: clean captions name the things on screen (white text and a thin pointer line), in English.

画风：2D 8-bit pixel art, a side view of a slice of skin like a textbook diagram come alive; 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; stepped 1-px outlines, a limited flat palette, at most a checker or ordered dither; no game elements (no HUD, titles, scores or player tags).
色板：#86D3EE #EEE2C8 #F4B79F #D97A80 #B95E6B #FFF1B8
角色：无（no characters and no faces: the cell we follow is marked only by a warmer tint and a light rim）

色板 = clear light-blue water, pale cream dead skin, peach living cells, rosy basal cells, the soft rose dermis, and the warm light rim on the cell we follow. Supporting tones: the lipid "mortar" between dead cells, dark granules, nuclei, pale collagen fibres, one small capillary with red cells; inside the dividing cell, a light pink nucleus, four chromosomes in four colours (maroon, gold, teal, green: both copies of one chromosome share its colour), cream spindle fibres, a pink ring that pinches the cell, orange mitochondria.
