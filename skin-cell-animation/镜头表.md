# 镜头表 · The Life Cycle of a Skin Cell

35 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 96 BPM, 14 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2.5 s). One cell, one slice of skin, one camera that never cuts away. The structure comes from the client's scenario: a flake of dead skin lifts off in flowing water (the hook); the camera dives back into the skin to where it came from; a basal cell divides; the new cell is pushed up through the layers. Part way up, the slice holds still for 4 bars and the camera zooms into the new cell, through its membrane, to show briefly how it does its job: its DNA holds the recipe for keratin, an RNA copy carries the recipe out of the nucleus, ribosomes build keratin from it, the keratin forms fibres that grip the neighbouring cells at the desmosomes, and the mitochondria power it all. The camera then comes back out the same way and the story goes on: the cell changes, dies and becomes a flat tile; the bonds holding the top tiles break and they peel away; the camera settles back on the opening shot, where our cell is now the flake about to lift off, so the last frame runs into the first. No narration: clean captions name the things on screen (white text and a thin pointer line), in English.

画风：2D 8-bit pixel art, a side view of a slice of skin like a textbook diagram come alive; 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; stepped 1-px outlines, a limited flat palette, at most a checker or ordered dither; no game elements (no HUD, titles, scores or player tags).
色板：#86D3EE #EEE2C8 #F4B79F #D97A80 #B95E6B #FFF1B8
角色：无（no characters and no faces: the cell we follow is marked only by a warmer tint and a light rim）

色板 = clear light-blue water, pale cream dead skin, peach living cells, rosy basal cells, the soft rose dermis, and the warm light rim on the cell we follow. Supporting tones: the lipid "mortar" between dead cells, dark granules, nuclei, pale collagen fibres, one small capillary with red cells; inside the cell, a light pink nucleus, gold RNA, maroon ribosomes, cream keratin and orange mitochondria.
