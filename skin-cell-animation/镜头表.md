# 镜头表 · The Life Cycle of a Skin Cell

35 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 96 BPM, 14 bars of 4/4 (one bar = 2.5 s). One cell's story told as a loop, in one camera move that never cuts away. The structure comes from the client's scenario (a skin cell is born at the bottom, rises, dies and flakes off, and it loops), reordered at the client's request so that the cell division runs from the very first frame: the film opens inside a basal cell that is already dividing and follows the division step by step for 20 s. The DNA, already copied, packs into chromosomes; a spindle catches them and lines them up; each chromosome splits and one copy goes to each end; a ring pinches the cell into two cells, one above the other. The camera comes back out as the top cell, ours, pops up; a fast-forward carries it up through the layers to the top, where it dies and becomes a flat tile, and lifts off in a flake into the water. The camera dives back down to the bottom cell, which stayed in the basal layer and is about to divide again, and zooms into it: the next frame is the first frame. No narration: clean captions name the things on screen (white text and a thin pointer line), in English.

画风：2D 8-bit pixel art, a side view of a slice of skin like a textbook diagram come alive; 270×480 canvas scaled exactly 4× nearest-neighbour; stepped 1-px outlines, a limited flat palette, at most a checker or ordered dither; no game elements (no HUD, titles, scores or player tags).
色板：#86D3EE #EEE2C8 #F4B79F #D97A80 #B95E6B #FFF1B8
角色：无（no characters and no faces: the cell we follow is marked only by a warmer tint and a light rim）

色板 = clear light-blue water, pale cream dead skin, peach living cells, rosy basal cells, the soft rose dermis, and the warm light rim on the cell we follow. Supporting tones: the lipid "mortar" between dead cells, dark granules, nuclei, pale collagen fibres, one small capillary with red cells; inside the dividing cell, a light pink nucleus, four chromosomes in four colours (maroon, gold, teal, green: both copies of one chromosome share its colour), cream spindle fibres, a pink ring that pinches the cell, orange mitochondria.
