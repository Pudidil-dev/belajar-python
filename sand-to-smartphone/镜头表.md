# 镜头表 · Shot list — FROM SAND TO SMARTPHONE

Silent 8-bit pixel-art explainer, 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, 195 s (3:15), 120 BPM grid (1 beat = 0.5 s, 1 bar = 2 s).
30 scenes (S01–S30), 41 shots. The camera follows one material the whole way: a quartz grain → silicon → crystal → wafer →
transistors → die → package → motherboard → smartphone, and ends back on the same sand dune.

## Accuracy notes kept on screen or in the motion

- Sand is not melted into wafers: SiO₂ is reduced with carbon (S04–S05), purified as trichlorosilane (S07–S08), and only then melted for crystal growth (S09–S11).
- EUV light changes resist chemistry only (S20); develop, etch, strip, implant and deposition make the structures (S21–S22, S24).
- Not every layer is EUV: S20 tags EUV as the critical-layer tool and S22 notes DUV 193 nm for other layers.
- The transistor is a clearly stylized gate-all-around nanosheet device (S23), not a claim about every chip.
- The die is packaged (flip-chip + memory package on top) before it is soldered to the phone board (S27–S28).
- Power comes from the battery through the power-management IC (S29); the chip does not make its own energy.
