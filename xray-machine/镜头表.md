# 镜头表 · Shot list — HOW X-RAYS ARE MADE

Silent 8-bit pixel-art explainer, 1080×1920 vertical, 60 fps, 65 s. Music grid 120 BPM: 1 beat = 0.5 s, 1 bar = 2 s.
Every scene boundary of the brief (0, 4, 8, 13, 18, 22, 29, 34, 38, 42, 47, 52, 57, 62, 65 s) falls on a beat, and the
first drop lands on bremsstrahlung (22 s), the second on the image build (57 s).

One continuous journey, never a static diagram: HAND → MACHINE → VACUUM TUBE → FILAMENT → ACCELERATING ELECTRONS →
TUNGSTEN ATOM → PHOTON → TISSUE → BONE → DETECTOR → SIGNAL → PIXEL IMAGE → FULL RADIOGRAPH.
Every particle has a physical trajectory: electrons boil off the hot filament, accelerate in the field, bend around
screened tungsten nuclei (precomputed Coulomb orbits) and lose exactly the energy their photon carries.
Each scene has its caption (the brief's wording) plus one short context line, and small event notes appear where and
when each event happens.

Fixed physics for the whole film: tube at 80 kV (each electron arrives with 80 keV, about half the speed of light);
tungsten Z = 74, K-shell binding 69.5 keV, Kα 59 keV, Kβ 67 keV; under 1% of the beam energy becomes X-rays.

画风：8-bit 像素画（270×480 低分辨率画、4 倍最近邻放大、全片一套限色调色板＋4×4 有序抖动），用 Kurzgesagt 的扁平分面、发光粒子、剖面揭开和对数尺度推拉组织画面与运动；近黑底，粒子发光但边缘是硬像素
色板：#05060c #3fe4ff #ffc93a #ff7a1a #b366ff #f2ead8
角色：只出手（病人的一只手：侧面、剖面和 X 光片），不出脸
允许蓝紫：用户点名「X-ray photons: bright violet」；紫色只用在光子和射束上，底色仍是近黑

Camera words: 停 hold · 缓推 slow push · 拉远 pull out · 横移 pan/track · 快推 fast push (accent) · 砸入 slam · 切 cut.
