## 制作说明（制作者填写；只写在这一段，事实段不要动）

- 用了哪个语法 / 风格:
  - **Style.** The y1 Kurzgesagt grammar drawn in 8-bit pixels, on the same 14_8bit pipeline as the two previous films
    in this repo:
    - every frame is drawn at 270×480 with one palette, 4×4 Bayer dither and a 4× nearest-neighbour upscale (60 fps);
    - the colour code is the brief's: cyan electrons, golden tungsten, orange nuclei, violet photons, red/orange heat,
      pale bone, pink tissue, cyan/green circuits, on a near-black background.
  - **One journey, three kinds of world.** The camera never leaves the action. The film moves between:
    - the room and the tube, in millimetres, with cutaways;
    - one shared tungsten lattice for scenes 5–8, where the camera pushes, pulls back and pans between atoms;
    - the hand and the detector, in millimetres.
  - **Scale jumps** are log zooms bridged by dither dissolves at matched framing.
  - **Physics drives the particles:**
    - screened Coulomb trajectories, integrated at load time;
    - the photon energy equals the electron's energy loss;
    - tungsten's binding energies for the characteristic lines;
    - a radiograph computed by integrating attenuation through the hand's geometry;
    - photon fates in the cross-section drawn from each photon's real path.
  - **Shot list.** `镜头表.md`: 17 shots, lint yellow (one item, explained below). No three-direction check was made,
    because the style direction is the one already used for the previous films in this repo; only the subject is new.
