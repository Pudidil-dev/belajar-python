# 镜头表 · Shot list — THE HEART IS STILL

*How a Heart–Lung Machine Works.* A 68-second vertical explainer for Reels / TikTok that works on mute.
- **Format.** 1080×1920 (9:16), exactly 68.000 s at 30 fps (2,040 frames): 20 scenes at the brief's timestamps, transitions included in each scene's time.
- **Pixels.** Drawn at 360×640 and upscaled exactly 3× with nearest-neighbour; one limited palette; dither only in the thin strip where two colour bands meet (codixel).
- **Text.** One short English caption at a time, drawn by the editor layer (`CAP`); `?notext=1` renders the clean version; `docs/labels.srt` lists every caption for re-editing.
- **Sound.** No narration. 120 BPM in 4/4, 34 bars = 68 s, synthesized in code; a continuous bed of operating-room hum, machine hum and nonpulsatile flow runs under every cut and across the loop seam. No heartbeat, no alarms. Music, effects and mix are separate stems.
- **Loop.** Scene 20 eases into the exact opening frame; the opening caption returns 0.2 s after the restart.

## The questions, in order

1. Why is blood moving while the heart is still? (S01–S04: a machine is moving it.)
2. Where does that blood go? (S05–S06: by gravity into a reservoir.)
3. How does the pump move it? (S07: a spinning rotor in a centrifugal pump.)
4. How does the machine add oxygen? (S08–S11: across the membrane of hollow fibres.)
5. How does oxygen enter a red blood cell? (S12.)
6. What carries that oxygen? (S13: hemoglobin.)
7. How does the blood return to the body? (S14–S17: CO₂ out, filter, aorta, tissue.)
8. Who monitors this process? (S18: the perfusionist.)

## Locked scenario and circuit (one reference layout for every scene)

- **Patient.** Adult, anesthetized, anticoagulated, on established cardiopulmonary bypass from frame one. The heart has already been arrested with cardioplegia for the chosen operation (an arrested-heart scenario, not a rule for all cardiac surgery). Median sternotomy shown as a clean, stylized cutaway window.
- **Venous side.** One two-stage venous cannula in the right atrium (the patient's right = the viewer's left) → venous line down the viewer's left → hard-shell venous reservoir, below the patient (gravity drainage).
- **Machine, left to right along the bottom.** Reservoir → centrifugal pump on its magnetic drive → integrated assembly (heat exchanger, hollow-fibre oxygenator, arterial filter) → arterial line with a clamp-on bubble detector → up the viewer's right side.
- **Arterial side.** Arterial cannula in the distal ascending aorta, on the systemic side of the aortic cross-clamp. A thin, subdued cardioplegia line reaches the aortic root on the heart side of the clamp.
- **Separate systems.** Gas supply (pale cyan outline) to the oxygenator's gas inlet, exhaust at its gas outlet; temperature-control water (blue outline) between a heater–cooler unit and the heat exchanger. Gas, water and blood never share a channel.
- **Oxygenator design.** Gas flows inside the hollow fibres, blood flows around them.
